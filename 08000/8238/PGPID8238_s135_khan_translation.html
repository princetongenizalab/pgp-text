English Translation of Legal document: T-S Ar.39.189

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  2. The slaves report the death of ʿAlāʾ, daughter of Bū al-ʿAlāʾ ibn Bū Saʿd [on]
  3. Thursday, the twenty-ninth of Dhū al-Qaʿda, in the year six-hundred and thirty-two.
  4. She left the following heirs: Her aforementioned father, Bū al-ʿAlāʾ, and herbrothers,
  5. Hiba and Bū Saʿd, the Sons of the aforementioned Bū al-ʿAlāʾ.
  6. They wrote their signatures in (testimony of) this on the aforementioned date.
  7. Praise be to God, the eternal. Witness clauses
