English Translation of Letter: T-S 13J30.6

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate.
  2. O community of the Jews! Where are you in relation to
  3. God? You allow the rayyis to lodge Ibn
  4. al-Maṣmūdī in the compound (dār) that
  5. is behind the synagogue of the Iraqis,
  6. which they have bought adjoining
  7. the secret doorway, the place where
  8. the women of the Jews and their daughters ascend (to the women's gallery). May God
  9. the exalted burn the house of whoever lodges him
  10. and whoever does not prevent this misfortune.
  11. O Jews! You are inflicting dishonor on your families by means of Ibn
  12. al-Maṣmūdī. Where are you
  13. in relation to God? If you want
  14. to lodge him (there) or if the rayyis lodges him,
  15. close the secret doorway to
  16. the Jews so that God
  17. does not summon whoever does such a thing
  18. to the people.
  19. Where is your nobility? Your honor has departed 
  20. from you on account of him. Peace.
