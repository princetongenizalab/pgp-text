Verso, left side 
A decree has been made concerning the protection of the shipowners in al-Nastarāwiyya and the prevention of the requisitioning of their vessels
and the prevention of people from al-Ġarbiyya
from fishing along the shore.
Mark
A gracious decree.
At the top there is the motto (written) in the noble hand:
Praise be to God, lord of the universe.
