English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.30.175

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  2. A statement submitted by Boqṭor ibn Sisinne, the overseer in Badsā, of what is in his possession by way of the yield of the seed-advance
  3. from the produce of the ḵarājī year four-hundred and one, and what is in hand
  4. in his possession, according to what is testified by his statement to the prosperous office in the dār al-ʿizz,
  5. for this aforementioned year, and what remains in the name of the cultivators,  
  6. in their possession or as having been returned by them; also what was brought to him from the šarīf ʾIsmaʿīl ibn al-Qāsim 
  7. al-Jaʿfarī for the disposition of the karājī year four-hundred and two, what has been granted from this 
  8. for the seed-advance of the estate known as Badsā for the karājī year  four-hundred and two.
  9. and what has been transferred to ʿAbdallāh ibn Malik, the surveyor, for the karājī year four-hundred and two. 
  10. He has signed it and has pledged to submit it and return it to the warehouse of  the sultan in the estate
  11. known as Badsā. All of it is reckoned in the type of wayba known as 'the small'  [                           ]
  12. Of the various kinds of produce: Two-hundred and forty-nine, a half and an eighth.
  13. Wheat: Ninety-seven irdabbs. 
  14. What is in hand of this in the possession of Boqṭor ibn Sisinne: thirty-four and a half irdabbs. What has been returned of the produce of the year one: fifty-six, a third and an eighth irdabbs.
  15. What remains in the name of the cultivators: Six, a third and an eighth irdabbs.
