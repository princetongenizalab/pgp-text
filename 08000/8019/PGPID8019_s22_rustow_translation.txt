Recto
Abundant peace and may salvation draw near for my master and lord, light of my eyes and crown of
my head, my lord, my husband, the honored teacher Shelomo the scribe, m(ay) G(od) p(reserve him). Receive
abundant greetings from the Lord of Peace, and from me, Dona Soro, your wife,
and (from) your daughters Reina and Raḥel, and R. Moshe, your son-in-law, and Rivqa.
All of us together long to see your pleasant face
as one sees the face of God (cf. Gen. 33:10). We are astonished at you: how is it that you have not
sent us a reply to the several letters we sent you?
We have sent for you several times, asking you to come, and there is no answer and no heed (cf. 1 Kgs. 18:29).
If you can manage to arrange with the honored R. Shelomo the physician,
m(ay) G(od) p(reserve him), that you be exempt from the tax, your reward will be very great,
and this commandment will be greater than all the kindnesses he has done for you,
and God will repay him with a good reward in this world and in the world to come,
and may He grant us merit for the res(urrection) of the d(ead). Now let us return to the first matter: We have all come,
we, your wife, your daughters, and Moshe, your son-in-law, to entreat you
very strongly not to go far away from us, neither by sea nor by land.
For we have heard that you intend to go to Turkey. By the life of the Lord of Hosts,
if you do this thing, you will not speak with us (again). Do you want
to do something for which the whole world will scorn us, and to sow
strife between your son-in-law and your daughter, who is pregnant?
Your daughter will be sorrowful, and perhaps she will miscarry, may it not happen to us. You will also ruin
the fortune of Raḥel, your daughter, who is grown, beautiful, good
and modest. For people will say: "Look, a good man, old,
and a scribe, abandoned his wife and daughters after so many years. Can it be other than that he has gone mad,
since he is going to a distant land?" You
know what the verse says: "The eyes of a fool are on the ends of
the earth" (Prov. 17:24). Therefore, entreat the honoured R. Shelomo the physician,
m(ay) G(od) p(reserve him), to [obtain] for you a document concerning the tax, so that you are exempt. If
not, come, blessed be the Lord, may we carry his children well, and do not do
anything else. Farewell.
