Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S NS J131

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. In Your name
  2. I had previously sent a letter to my lord the elder Dear One of the Community, may God prolong his existence  

Recto, right margin

  1. grains of the Fayyūm

Recto, top margin

  1. ...
  2. ...
  3. I had mentioned to him (i.e., you) in my previous letter
  4. the situation of our coreligionists the glassmakers
  5. who are in the Fayyūm and the worry that has flowed into my heart
  6.  on their account constantly. I had asked him (i.e., you) —
  7. and I now renew my request of him —
  8. to mediate among them

Verso, address, right

  1. My lord the elder Abū Isḥāq Ibrāhīm
  2. b. [   ] our teacher Natan,
  3. may God prolong his existence and make his glory, support, and benefactions eternal

Verso, address, left

  1. The one who loves him, Mevorakh bar Saʿadya, of blessed memory.
