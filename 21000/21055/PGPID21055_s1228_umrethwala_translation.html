English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.41.100

Yusuf Umrethwala, Unpublished translations (n.p., 2026).

Recto

  1. [. . . . . . . . .]

  2. leads to the deliverance [. . . . . .] what honors them. As for what you mentioned concerning the condition of [. . . . . . . .]

  3. and his gratitude from Constantine, for the [. . .] of the farmers has returned(?) and none remain except them. The cause of this 

  4. is therefore not known. He should choose to exercise caution/surround regarding all that belongs to them in the district — crops/yields, fiscal agents, and other matters — with the utmost vigilance,

  5. and inquire thoroughly into their condition. And if suspicion appears to you, then they have ordered the arrest of Mughāra(?) son of [. . .]

  6. [and] detention and pressure upon him, he being the one who carries out everything they command; and nothing was accepted from him

  7. ] the justice upon them in full and complete measure, and he is not to be granted leniency(?).

Right margin

  1. And it is necessary that [. . .]

  2. the totality of what he mentioned(?) be hastened in being conveyed, and that attention be devoted to

  3. the collection of money and the full collection of the kharāj,

  4. ] upon it from it, and that no effort be spared that may lead to

  5. ] the conduct of an ignorant person.
