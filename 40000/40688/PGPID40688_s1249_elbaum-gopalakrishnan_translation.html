English Translation of Literary text: ENA NS 30.21

Alan Elbaum and Pratima Gopalakrishnan, Unpublished Editions.

  1. Place the water used to extract the bran into [. . .] 
  2. Take some of the ground lac with the sticks stripped away. Kn[ead it with some] of the
  3. clarified bran water. Grind it in a mortar, as a paste, very finely until it becomes like 
  4. an ointment. Extract it with the bran water through a sieve of many holes until 
  5. the lac sediment runs clear. Take the silk that has been treated with forty 
  6. dirhams of splintered alum after it has soaked in the alum and water for a night and a day, 
  7. and proceed to wash off the alum and wring it. Take half of the water containing 
  8. the lac extract and place it in a copper vessel. Light a fire beneath it until it boils and splits, 
  9. then skim off the foam. Lower the silk into it and reduce the fire. Do not cease turning it
  10. until it drinks up (the dye) and the water becomes clear. Take it out and rinse it in ample water, and do not. . . .
