English Translation of Credit instrument or private receipt: T-S Ar.30.184.5

Ben Outhwaite, Discarded History: The Genizah of Medieval Cairo (Cambridge University Library, 2017).

In [your name, O Merciful One].

Truth. 5 dinars.

May the elder Abu’l-Khayr Khiyyar pay to the bearer five dinars for wax candles.

The month of Av, 1451 of the Seleucid Era.

Abū Zikrī Kohen
