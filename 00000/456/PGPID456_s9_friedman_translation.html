English Translation of Legal document: Yevr. III B 637

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. and a coat made of different materials—two dinars, five skullcaps—three dinars, [a ... half-mantle—one dinar]  
  2. and a half, three pillows—one dinar, a shawl—one and a half dinar. The sum total is eight dina[rs. And they stipulated]  
  3. between themselves that if this Mevorakh b. R. Japheth hates this Dalāl, the bride, daughter of Yaḥye, and does not [desire] 
  4. her partnership, and wants to divorce her, he will pay to her the balance of her mohar, from beginning to end,
  5. even from the shirt on his back. And we performed a complete qinyan, with a suitable instrument, with Mevorakh b. Japheth,  
  6. the groom, (to affirm) that he not alter the law of the Rabbanites on all their festivals and fast days, and all of their laws. And she will conduct herself  
  7. with him according to his law, and this Dalāl daughter of Yaḥye will not desecrate his law in his presence. They took upon themselves the security of this  
  8. deed, according to the stringencies of the deeds established by the sages. We performed with them a complete strict qinyan with an instrument suitable  
  9. for this for all that is written and specified. Valid and trustworthy in whatever court it is produced and read,  
  10. whether in the laws of the gentiles or the statutes of our lords and sages. In good order. 
  11. Obadiah ha-Ko[hen ...]. Jephthah ha-Kohen b. R. Joseph (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) b. Amram (whose) s(oul is at) r(est).
  12. Jo[seph(?) ...
