On the fourth day (of the week), in the month of Nisan, fourteen days therein, the sixth year of the sabbatical cycle, which is y[ear f]our thousand,  
eight hundred and forty-three A.M., in the town of Damsīs, situated on the Nile river: Eli b. Aaron (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) \\the kohen//  [declared]
voluntarily, willingly, and of his own choice, not coerced, not distressed, not enticed, nor intoxicated to take this lady  
Ḥisān, the virgin and bride, daughter of m(aster) Jacob the kohen (whose) s(oul is at) r(est)—to esteem honor, nourish, provide for, and clothe [he]r, in the manner of Jewish  
men, who nourish, provide for, and clothe their wives, the daughters of Israel, in cleanness and purity. Similarly responded Ḥisān, the virgin and bride,
daughter of m(aster) Jacob, the kohen, who is at rest, that she would esteem, honor and attend him, in the manner of modest women, who esteem, honor, serve and attend  
their husbands, the sons of Israel, in purity and sanctity. And he effected her qiddushin with a cup of wine and a silver ring. And they fixed the mohar between them at thirty [dinars],  
comprised of ten dinars that are advanced and twenty full dinars, in royal coinage, that are delayed—after he had given her twenty-five [the mohar]  
of virgins, in silver zuz, in agreement with what the sages of Israel had expressed: "Not, less than two hundred is given for a virgin and one hundred for a widow." And the t[wo] sides  
agreed on this mohar. These are what she brought, in with her with her with the assistance of the Merciful: a pair of gold earrings–three dinars,  
a pair of bangles–three dinars, a pair of bracelets–two dinars, a white wimple–two dinars, two linen shirts–two dinars,
a shawl–one and a quarter dinars, a woman’s scarf and an Ashkelon headdress–one dinar, a mat–one dinar, seven c[ushions?]–one dinar, two skullcaps–
one dinar, a sleeping mat–one dinar, a pair of pants–one half dinar and necessities–one fourth dinar. The total of all [which] she brought in: [nine-]teen 
dinars. They will be for her and her son after her. This girl is of age and in her own jurisdiction. This girl, the bride, appointed Shela, the kohen, b. R.
Jacob, the kohen who is at rest, as her agent and to care for her affair, by the word of two witnesses who were acquainted with her. And [thes]e are their names: Aaron,
the elder, b. R. Barukh, (who) r(ests in) E(den), and Sason b. Eleazar, of Aleppo, r(emembered) for (good). Eli, the groom, \\the kohen// b. Aaron, (whose) s(oul is at) r(est), and Ḥisān, the virgin, the bride, daughter of Jacob, [the] kohen, agreed
