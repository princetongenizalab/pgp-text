English Translation of Legal document: Yevr. III B 164

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. [...] GROOM AND BRIDE. MAY THEY BUILD AND PROSPER  
  2. [... hun]dred and one years of the era of documents, in the city of Damascus, which is located on the rivers Amanah and Parpar the jurisdiction of ou[r] lord [...] 
  3. [...] the head of the Academy of the Pride of Jacob, (may the) m(emory of the) p(ious one) and (the saint) be (a blessing), this marriage contract was written: (Our) Master Uzziel, the esteemed young man [...]came before us [and declared …]  
  4. [...] voluntari[ly], and of his own choice, not being coerced, nor constrained, nor enticed, nor intoxicated, rather with full faculties and c[lear] speech [...]
  5. ["... to be to me wi]fe and the mother of children, according to the law of Moses and Israel, in complete qiddushin. I will serve, nourish, support, sustain, and maintain [...]  
  6. [ ... their wives fai]thful[ly]." And this bride listened to him (agreed) and willingly consented to be married to him and serve him in the manner of respectable wives [...]  
  7. [who ... their husbands, in cleanness and puri]ty. And since this bride is in her own jurisdiction, she willingly appointed her mother's brother, (the) ḥaver, the esteemed young man ⟦son of⟧, (our) master and l(ord) [...]  
  8. [...] She willingly consented to all that he does in her affair and stipulates on her behalf and as an obligation upon her. Two witness[es] performed with her a qinyan concerning this [...]  
  9. [...] her [di]nars, of which he advanced twenty dinars. They were included in her dowry. There remain incumbent upon him for [ty ...]
  10. [...] a strong and firm [debt]. She has no right to claim of them one peruṭa as long as he is alive and she is under his jurisdiction [...]

  12. [...]... a gold ring and two buckles—three dinars, a silver ring and a buckle a ... qaṭlūq ... in which are ten [...]
