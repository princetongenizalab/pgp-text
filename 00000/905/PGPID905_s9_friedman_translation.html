English Translation of Legal document: PER H 18

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. By the name of the Merciful. May His name be blessed forever.
  2. [On the ... day] of the week, in the month of Adar, fourteen days therein, of the year 958 of the era of the Temple, may it be (God's) will that it be rebuilt in [our] days, [in the city of Aleppo]
  3. [which is] situated on the river Qoueiq. "I, Ṣedaqa ha-Levi b. Yeshuʿa, the Jew, the groom—I request you, thou Ḥusn daughter of Wahb, the Jewess [...]
  4. [... to] be my wife. And I will give you your mohar and ketubba, valued in money as 200 silver zuz, as Moses instructed to give to the virgins, the daughters of Isra[el ...]
  5. [any v]ow, adjuration, oath, and bond, which are (will be) upon you." [And] this bride consented to be married to this, her husband, and to serve him in piety and clean[ness ...]
  6. [...] "I will supply you with your food and your clothing, as other Jewish husbands who maintain their wives faithfully. And if you are taken captive, I will ransom [you with my possessions, exclusive]
  7. [of] your ketubba [mon]ey, and I will take you back as my wife, as at first. And male children which I may have by you (they) shall inherit your ketubba money beyo[nd their portion]
  8. [with] their brothers. And female children which you may have by me (they) shall dwell in my house and shall be sustained from my property until their time will arrive and I give them in marriage to [Jewish] men [...]
  9. [... And i]f I go before you to my eternal home, you shall dwell in my house and be sustained from my estate for the entire duration of your widowhood after me. And i[f ...]
  10. [...] And if my heirs desire to evict you, you shall take (for yourself) your ketubba (money)." The qiddush money by which he effected the marriage was fifty [dinars ...]
