  9. This is to inform the honorable elders that the bearer of this letter
  10. is a prominent personality from ʾArqa who suffered great calamities and
  11. many misfortunes. His children and wife were taken captive and he lost everything he possessed, remaining "naked
  12. and bare." "I went away full, and God has brought me back empty." He learned that his children
  13. and wife were alive. He searched after them from one place to another, but found
  14. only one boy and the wife, while the others had been murdered. The captives are under
  15. the hand of a man who stood security for them until they would be ransomed. This man
  16. was a benefactor to people, and now he travels seeking the mercy of God
  17. and the compassion of "the munificent from the people of the God of Abraham." They do all the time
  18. works of charity with him and deeds of lovingkindness. May God reward them.
  19. "For He is a God of recompense," He will requite. And "almsgiving saves
  20. from death," etc.
