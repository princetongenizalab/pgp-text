Recto
O my lord and master, full of favor toward me and whose word carries far […]
I watched for it out of reliance on the surplus of your good character traits, and am returning the mithqāl to you, since it is not
good. There will also reach you five hundred leaves of paper that were with me and with the son of
master ʿOvadya, who is now ill; they belong to the goods of Ibn ʿOvadya. […]
Be informed of this. I have no news except what has been arranged: my setting out toward you this
coming week, if God the Exalted decrees. The most perfect greeting
upon my lord and most exalted master, and the mercy of God.
Sorry, sorry!
