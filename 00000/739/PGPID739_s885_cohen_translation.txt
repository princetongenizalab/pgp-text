Please write thanking the elder, Abu ’l-Qāsim b. al-Ukhuwwa, for the favor he is doing us. Letters of our lord the Head of the Yeshiva arrived in two copies for our lord the Nagid—may God perpetuate his strength—and for the community—may God protect it.
We forwarded them, (one) with the caravan and the other in the ships. We, on our part, are in a situation, as God only knows, of controversy and conflict. These people have gotten the better of us by bribing the chief of police and preventing us from praying for our Head and from mentioning his name in our synagogue. If we were not afraid of what the Sultan might do to this person, we would certainly appeal for his help. His father-in-law and brothers-in-law have gone to the Chief Qāḍi—may God perpetuate his strength—and have suggested that he [assume jurisdiction over (?)] the pious foundations of the synagogues and over the community chest by having someone administer them on his behalf.
Furthermore, they suggested to him that all court cases between Jews be put under his control, and that no Jew be allowed to have jurisdiction over them. However, they were unsuccessful in this attempt. We are now fearful of what, God forbid, the Sultan may do. May God grant us and you peace and security, Peace to our lord the Nagid and peace to his sons and brothers, and to all his adherents. May he increase for ever and ever and for all eternity.
