English Translation of Legal document: ENA 4010.36 + PER H 1

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

verso

Ḥalfōn b. Aaron  

Ghāliya daughter of Musāfir 

recto

  1. By the name of our Creator. May they build and prosper.
  2. On the fifth day (of the week), the seventh day of the month of Elul, which is four thousand,  
  3. eight hundred and thirty-nine years [A.]M., this marriage contract [was written]
  4. in the city of Tyre, which is on the coast of the [great] sea. [Ḥalfōn b.  Aa]ron, (whose) s(oul is at) r(est), [came] and said,  
  5. intentionally and will[ingly ...]  
  6. [...]  
  7. in the manner of Jewish men, who [nou]rish, [su]stain, and [...]
  8. and honor their wives faithfully. And this Ghāliya [...] listened to him (accepted his proposal)  
  9. and consented to be married to him and to esteem and honor him, to attend and to serve [him in the manner of]  
  10. respectable women, the daughters of Israel, who esteem, honor, attend, and serve  
  11. their husbands in purity and cleanness. This Ghāliya appointed her brother, Ḥalfōn b. Musā[fir],  
  12. to be her agent for the receipt of her qiddush (betrothal gift) from the hands of this Ḥalfōn b. Aaron.  
  13. And she willingly consented to all he does concerning her match and what he stipulates on behalf of her and as an obligation upon her. And there testified  
  14. to her two witnesses, who are acquainted with her. The mohar that this Ḥalfōn, the groom, set  
  15. for this Ghāliya, the bride, is twelve dinars. Two dinars he gave as a first payment [...]; and there remain  
  16. incumbent upon him ten perfect, weighed dinars, of the dinars of the state, delayed as a debt upon him,  
  17. sound and firm. She has no right to claim of them one peruṭa as long as he  
  18. is alive and she is under his jurisdiction and he fulfills her three needs. But if he hates her without  
  19. any misconduct (on her part) and does not fulfill her three needs, he shall pay them to her, viz. the balance of her mohar, of
  20. twelve dinars, and release her with a bill of divorce. And these are what she brought with her from the Merciful and from 
  21. the house of her fathers: a pair of gold earrings—2 dinars, a wrap—2  dinars, a coat made of different materials—
  22. ¼ dinar, a skullcap—1 dinar, a skullcap and a gown—2 dinars, a "half-garment" and a slip—  
  23. 2 dinars, a wrap— 1 ½ dinars, three Ṭabarī cushions and a pair of ʿAbbadānī mats—  
  24. 1 dinar, a chest and a bench—1 dinar, a basin and a pitcher—1 dinar, a lamp— 
  25. 2 dinars, and gold—2 dinars. The sum total is 16 dinars and ½ 
  26. dinar and ¼ dinar. And they are for her and her son after her. And if she passes away without leaving  
  27. sons or daughters by him, what remains will be divided into two equal portions.  
  28. One portion will be acquired by her husband, and the other portion will return to the house of her fathers. And with this  
  29. understanding, the two sides stipulated and agreed between themselves concerning hate and love  
  30. and life and death and all the stipulations of the Torah and the court stipulations from beginning to end. And they took  
  31. upon themselves the obligation of this marriage contract according to the stringencies of all valid documents,  
  32. verified in court, not like promises and not like formularies. And we performed with them  
  33. a complete qinyan, with an implement fit for doing so, on all that is written and specified  
  34. above. This marriage contract is established, verified, and trustworthy wherever it is produced and read,  
  35. whether according to the laws of the nations of the world or according to the statutes of our lords, the sages. May they build and prosper.
  36. Amram ha-Kohen b. R. Aaron (may his) e(nd be) g(ood). Abraham b. R. Joshua he-ḥaver (who) r(ests in) E(den).  
  37. Japheth b. Abraham b. Siman-Tav (may his) e(nd be)g(ood). Samuel b. R. Nathan m(ay his) s(oul be) b(ound) i(n the bundle of) l(ife).
  38. Amram he-ḥaver b. Ṣedaqa (who) r(ests in) E(den).
