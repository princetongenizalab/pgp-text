Recto
The complaint reached us regarding the benefit we had from him. [gap]
The sijill mukarram came down from the glorious presence, mentioning what
Sibāʿ ibn Faraj the Jew had reported concerning the condition of the Jewish dhimmīs
in Tiberias, that among them are those who pay for their jizya five dirhams
at a fixed rate, and others who pay ten dirhams, and that some of them claim
to be Khaybarī. This was the reason for his covetousness toward us, strengthening
his hand in oppressing us, and demanding payment from us through assessment at the highest jizya,
and meanwhile we have receipts going back many years indicating what each person pays.
