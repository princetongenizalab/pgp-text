Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of State document: ENA NS 18.26

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. The complaint reached us regarding the benefit we had from him. [gap]
  2. The sijill mukarram came down from the glorious presence, mentioning what
  3. Sibāʿ ibn Faraj the Jew had reported concerning the condition of the Jewish dhimmīs
  4. in Tiberias, that among them are those who pay for their jizya five dirhams
  5. at a fixed rate, and others who pay ten dirhams, and that some of them claim
  6. to be Khaybarī. This was the reason for his covetousness toward us, strengthening
  7. his hand in oppressing us, and demanding payment from us through assessment at the highest jizya,
  8. and meanwhile we have receipts going back many years indicating what each person pays.
