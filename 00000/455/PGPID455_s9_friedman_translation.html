English Translation of Legal document: Yevr. III B 635

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. By the name of our Creator.  
  2. On the third day (of the week), in the month of Av, nineteen days therein, fifth year of the sabbatical cycle, which is [...] 
  3. years A.M., in the city of Tyre, which is on the shore of the great sea, [this] marria[ge] deed was written [... Manṣūr]  
  4. b. m(aster) Joseph ha-ʿIbillanī (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) [came] and declared in our presence, willingly, intentionally, voluntarily, and of [his ow]n choice [... and not]  
  5. intoxicated, [ra]th[er] with full faculties and clear speech—to marry this Karīma, (who is) single, the divorcee, daughter of m(aster) [Hillel  ...]  
  6. to clothe, esteem and honor her in the manner of decent Jewish men who nourish, clothe, esteem [... And she accepted]  
  7. his proposal, this Karīma, and willingly consented to be married to him and to esteem and honor him, to attend and serve h[im ... in the manner of the daughters]  
  8. of Israel, who honor, esteem, attend and serve ... faithfully, in purity and cleanness, in piety [... She appointed X]  
  9. b. m(aster) Amram b. m(aster) ... , as her agent, to give her in marriage to m(aster) Manṣūr, the groom … [... She accepted all he does] 
  10. in her affair and what he stipulates for her and binding upon her. And two witnesses who are acquainted with her testified to her. [... And the marriage gift of this Karīma]  
  11. the daughter of Hillel is twenty fully weighed dinars, of the dinars of the state, of which he advanced five d[inars, and there remain]
  12. fifteen fully weighed dinars, delayed, incumbent upon him. She has no right to demand of them [one] peruṭa [as long as he is alive and she is under]  
  13. his jurisdiction, well-off, not hungry nor thirsty. And these are what she brought in with her from the Merciful and from her pa[ternal] home: [X fully weighed dinars,]  
  14. of the dinars of the state—this Manṣūr, the groom, acknowledged having received payment of them; they entered his possession and his jurisdiction, ... to be used] 
  15. in his work, for as long as this Karīma is under his jurisdiction; a pair of gold bangles—fifteen dinars; [...]
  16. four dinars, two pearl ḥabish(?)—1 dinar; a copper lamp—two dinars;  a basin and a pitcher [...]  
  17. one and a half dinars; three water containers, a serving-tray, and a  jug—one and a half dinars; a qashwa—one dinar; two blue linen coats  [...] 
  18. a Saʿīdī bed cover and a carpet and seven Ṭabarī cushions—three and a half dinars; [...]  
  19. dinars; a makhtūma of siglaṭun fabric and a fine linen gown—three d[inars; ...]  
  20. three cloaks—two dinars; a tabl[e ...]  
  21. dinars. The sum total of what she brought in with her [...]
