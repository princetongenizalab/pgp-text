English Translation of Letter: ENA 2805.26

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. In Your Name
  2. I am writing to my lord and master, may God lengthen your life and perpetuate your well-being and happiness, and divert
  3. misfortunes from you through His mercy, from Alexandria with 14 (days) remaining in Tammuz, in a state of health.
  4. prosperity, and longing for you. May God bring about a reunion with you in the best circumstances. What
  5. you need to know is that the reason I haven't written to you all this time is only that I
  6. was away in the Buḥayra (district) until I obtained my shipment of wheat. I arrived here
  7. yesterday (at the time of) writing this letter. I met with my lord Abū l-Faraj Yeshuaʿ more than
  8. 12 days ago. He was going east with silk and processed wax that he was seeking to sell.
  9. I asked him whether he had taken with him for you the silk that belongs to you. He told me that the elder
  10. Abū Isḥaq had not handed anything over to him, and this distressed me. He mentioned that he had taken out
  11. the coral for you. Until now, I haven't succeded in meeting with the elder Abū Isḥaq to ask him
  12. what the reason was for this. I believe that by the time this reaches you, it will have already arrived before you,
  13. and he will inform you of the explanation of things and what we went through during the packing season. May God the exalted make

Verso

 

  1. buy the wheat for my provisions only because I had a large amount deposited in .... I sold silk that was
  2. with me. My lord Abū l-Faraj Yeshuaʿ will inform you... As for what you asked about customs,
  3. I have sent the aloeswood with Abū l-Khayr. I hope they arrive safely. My letter
  4. will reach you after that, with an explanation of additional news about the ships. They came to us
  5. ... I hope, God willing, that they have moved. I send the most perfect greetings to you. To my lord Abū l-Faraj,
  6. your son-in-law, I send perfect greetings. To my master the Rav, perfect greetings. To my lord Abū Sulaymān Dāwūd and his young man, perfect greetings.

  8. I have sent you one dīnār of al-Mahdiyya from what was paid to Ibn Shamʿūn/Shimʿon, and 3 dīnārs of Aleppo with Yonah. I have informed you of this. Peace.
