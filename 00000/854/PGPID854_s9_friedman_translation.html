Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. [On the fifth day,] in the month of Kislev, twelve days therein, in the th[ird] year [of the sabbatical cycle, which is year four thousand,]
  2. [seven hundred and] eighty-four A.M., in the city of [Tyre, was written]  
  3. [this marriage [contact]: Nathan ha-Kohen of Safed b. Solomon, (may his) e(nd be) g(ood), b.'[...], came [and said, "Intentionally, willingly,]  
  4. [voluntarily, and] of my own choice, and not being coerced, not constrained, and not pressed, [... rather]  
  5. [... with] full [senses] and clear speech, I desire, of my own will, to marry [my] betrothed [...]  
  6. [Rachel daughter of...] the [ḥa]zzan, (may his) e(nd be) g(ood), son of our master Abraham the ḥazzan, (whose) s(oul is at) r(est), that I might bri[ng] her into my house, so that she will be [my wife ... on condition]  
  7. [that I hono]r her, feed her, sustain her, esteem [her], as all [... in the  man]ner of dece[nt Jewish] men, [who...]  
  8. [... fa]ithfully. I have fixed as an obligation for her incumbent upon me qidd[ushin...] fifty dinar[s...]  
  9. [... and fi]ve dinars, fully weighed, pure go[ld ... o]f which five fully weighed (coins) are [the] adv[anced payment. And there remain incumbent upon me]  
  10. [...] a strong firm [debt]." And [this] l[a]dy Rachel, the virgin, listened to him (accepted his proposal)] and took upon [herself to be married]  
  11. [to him ...] according to the laws of Moses and Israel. And she  undertook to honor, esteem, attend and [serve him ... in the manner of]  
  12. [dece]nt [women], the daughters of Israel, who attend and serve their husbands in purity and san[ctity....]  
  13. [And this girl] has reached her majority, with physical signs of maturity. She has attained puberty and is in her own jurisdiction. She has appointed [our] ma[ster ...]  
  14. [... to b]e her agent for the business of her mohar. And she consented to all that he do[es.... There testified to her before us]
  15. [two] fit [witnesses] who are acquainted with her—and for the receipt of her qiddush money. And this our master [...] acknowledged
  16. [that he had received] the [adv]anced payment of her mohar, five dinars, the qiddush money of this Rachel, his daughter, and [...]
  17. [...] And he (the groom) brought her into his home and took her in complete qiddushin, by money, by writ, and by [intercourse ...] 
  18. [... trustworthy, pious. And there remain incumbent upon him twenty fully weighed, pure gold dinars, of the dinar[s of the state ...]
  19. [...as the del]ayed payment, against him. She has no right to claim them from him as long as he is alive [and she is under]
  20. [his jurisdiction], well-off, not hungry, nor thirsty. And these are what this Rachel brought in from the house [of her fathers ...]
  21. [to the house of] her [hus]band: gold bracelets known as aswira—twelve d[inar]s, [a pa]ir of gold earrings [...]  
  22. [...] two silver bangles—three dinars, silver anklets known as khalākhil—eight [dinars...]  
  23. [...]—one dinar, a copper pail and a shallow drinking vessel—one  dinar, a short-cut shirt—two d[inars...]  
  24. [... dinar]s, two colored shirts and a skullcap two dinars, three pillows  know[n as makhādd...]
  25. [...]—one [di]nar, a shirt and two scarves—one dinar, a shirt—one  dinar, two larg[e] sheets—[...]  
  26. [... dinar]s, three protective head coverings—one dinar, a colored skullcap and coat—two dinars, two skullcaps [...]  
  27. [...] two [s]hirts and a skullcap—two dinars, a colored quilt coverlet—one dinar, a copper lamp [...]  
  28. [These are wh]at this girl brought in from the Merciful and from the house of her fathers. They total for her to a number of fo[rty-...]
  29. [dinar]s and one third dinar. And they are for her and for her son after her. If she passes from the world in her husband's life, [her son shall]
  30. [acquire] whatever remains of these articles. And if, God forbid, she passes from the world without [children, half shall return to the house of]  
  31. her [fathers.] And they stipulated between themselves concerning  hate and love and life and death—If this Natha[n, the groom, hates]
  32. [this] Rachel, his wife, and does not desire her partnership—a gratuitous hatred, he shall pay her all that is wr[itten and specified in this]  
  33. marriage [contract], completely. And if this Rachel, the bride, hates this Nathan, her husband, and does not desire his partnership, she shall]  
  34. [los]e the delayed payment of her mohar and shall take what she brought in, and she shall not leave, except by the authorization of [the] cou[rt. "And if]  
  35. [I go to] my eternal [h]ome before you, female children [that] you will have by me (they) shall dwell in my house and shall be [supported from my estate]  
  36. [until] they are married to decent J[e]wish men. And you shall dwell in my house and shall be provided for from my estate [for the duration]  
  37. of your [widowhoo]d, until my heirs choose to pa[y you] your ketub a [mo]ney." And he stipulated for her: "If you go mad, [...] 
  38. [...] your ketubba money. And if you are taken captive, I will rans[om you] and I will return you to your city. And if you leave [the] w[orld before me,]  
  39. male [children] that I shall have by you (they) shall inherit your ketubba money beyond their share which [they] shall inh[erit with]
  40. [the]ir [brothers], which I may have from another wife"— and all the court stipulations from beginning to end. And th[is Nathan,]
  41. [the groom,] acknowledged that he received in full all that is written  and specified in this marriage contract. And he said: ["I am] obligated to make payment,]  
  42. [whether myself] or from my heirs who will remain after me. And all things that I possess and that I shall acquir[e, whether]
  43. [mo]vables or real estate, all of them will be surety and guaranties for payment of this marriage contract."  
  44. And the two sides [took upon] the[mselves] the responsibility and stringencies of this marriage contract according to the stringencies of val[id] deeds  
  45. [verified in court. And with this understanding the two sides concluded and consented, willingly and [...]
