English Translation of Legal document: Bodl. MS heb. c 13/16

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. By the Name [of our Creator]. Build and Prosper.
  2. [On] the fifth [da]y (of the week), twelve days in the month Sivan, in the sabbatical year, which is [four] 
  3. [thousand,] seven hundred and \\ninety//five years A.M. , in the city of Tiberias Colon[ia ...] 
  4. [... fore]ver(?). "I, Isaac b. m(aster) Abraham (whose) s(oul is at)  r(est), declare, intentionally, willingly, and of [my own]
  5. [choic]e, not being coerced, nor vexed, nor distressed, nor constrained, nor forced, nor enticed, [nor intoxicated, but rather]
  6. [wholeheartedly and with full senses, I desire to marry th[is] lady Nāʿima, who is single, daughter of [master Moses, the ḥazzan.]
  7. [And I s]hall nourish, provide for, clothe, esteem, and hono[r her in] the manner of Jewish men [who nourish,]  
  8. [provide for,] clothe, esteem and honor their wives faithfully." Also  sh[e ac]cepted and took [upon herself]  
  9. [that she will serv]e, attend, esteem, and honor her husband in the manner of de[c]ent women, the daughters [of Israel,] 
  10. [who serve,] attend, esteem, and honor their husbands in purity and cleanness. And she consented and became his w[ife and the mother]
  11. [of children, a]s he had de[sir]ed [and] requested, in accordance with the law of Moses and the Jews. This master Isaac b. m(aster) A[braham (whose) s(oul is at) r(est)] took in qiddushin
  12. [Nā]ʿima, who is single, daughter of master Moses, the ḥazzan, (whose) s(oul is at) r(est), with a silver ring. And they also fixed b[etween themselves a mohar of ...]
