English Translation of State document: T-S H5.115

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. ... new, and trustworthy regarding people and property, a name is required
  2. that gathers together all these epithets so that he may be distinguished with these qualities.

  4. [                        ] your satisfaction . . .
