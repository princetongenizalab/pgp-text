English Translation of Legal document: T-S 16.181

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. On the fifth day [of the week], twenty-eight days in the month of Adar II, year 3 of the sabbatical cycle, which is year 4 thousand, six hu[ndred]
  2. and ninety-three A.M., in Damascus, Eli b. Wuhayb married Hiba, the virgin, daughter of Jonah, and gave [to her]
  3. as mohar a choker and a pair of gold zaylaʿ ornaments—5 din. And there remain incumbent upon him twenty dinars,
  4. the balance of the mohar. She brought in from her father’s house: a pair of gold bracelets—30 din.,
  5. a shiny coat—5 din., a colored coat—3 din., a coat—all of it [?]‌ [ . . . ]
  6. . . . a shiny . . .—. din., an unpatterned kerchief—3 din., a col[ored] coat,
  7. four shirts—4 din., two covers—5 din., colored “bathing clothes”[?]—[.] din.,
  8. a rashīdī slip—2 din., a veil, a wimple and five kerchiefs—[.] din.,
  9. a Maisani [from the district of Maisān, in southern Iraq—Ed.] container with all its needs—twenty din., a flyswatter—15 din., a ra[shīdī] container—
  10. 25 din., two brocade bed covers—8 din., 6 velvet covers [?]—10 din., 10 pillows [. . .]
  11. 7 din., 2 curtains—6 din., a stand, an oven, a basin, two pails,
  12. a dyeing vessel, a pitcher, a lamp, a cooler, 11 cups, 11 jugs, a serving-tr[ay]
  13. all of them copper—40 din., a ẓarāʾif container—15 din., 4 chests—
  14. 8 din., 4 racks for slips—4 din., a box—2 din. [She] also brought in
  15. from her father’s house an apartment [house] which has two rooms, one ground level and one middle level,
  16. in the compound of Ibn Armīnī[?], in the eastern part of the compound. Their boundaries: to the south, [her] father’s rooms,
  17. to the east, the rooms of Aaron b. Naḥmī; to the north, the room of Ibn Lwrʾ[?]; and to the west
  18. the gate of the compound and the public thoroughfare. These rooms are [conveyed] with every right which they possess, exposed and con[cealed].
  19. These[?] [that] she brought in are 3 portions from 8 portions, the boundaries of which are set forth in this writ, mixed not divided. They are
  20. her mohar and dowry: two hundred and sixty-two dinars, excluding these portions. May they build and prosper.
  21. He added for her a sum of fifteen dinars a gift for mohar. Isaac b. Samuel b. Ni[hmi].
  22. Nissin b. Savoy, witness, testified. Joshua b. Sulḥ,
  23. whose soul is at rest, witness. Ezra b. Samuel b. Ezra testified to
  24. the acknowledgement of Eli the groom to all this in this wr[it], on the date given within. Aaron b.
  25. [Ben]jamin b. Nihmi, witness. Bishr b. Khalaf ha-Kohen witness[ed].
  26. [Ab]raham b. ʿUthman, witness. Ḥasan b. ʿImrān was a witness. And this was on the day of gathering.
  27. [Isaa]c b. Jacob, the Kohen and the scribe, witness.
