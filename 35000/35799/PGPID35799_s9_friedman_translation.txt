b. Isaac b. Saʿīd b. Pinhas gave the ring of security for Zuhra, the virgin, daughter of R. Jacob ha-Kohen, the scribe, according to

five, the mohar of virgins, and the rest as an addition for mohar. And he advanced at the time of this security
fifteen good, fully weighed gold pieces. And at the time that he marries her, he shall pay her thirty-five,  
the balance of the fifty. Rav Jacob, her father, accepted for her this ring of security, this [r]ing of security(!). He shall provide for
her [needs] until the time of her marriage to her husband. Valid is this writ of betrothal in the laws (courts) of Israel. May they build and prosper.
