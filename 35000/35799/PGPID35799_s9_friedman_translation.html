English Translation of Legal document: T-S 16.181

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. On the 5th of the week, 16 days in the month of Adar I, year 3 of the sabbatical cycle, an interculated year, which is year  
  2. 4,693 A.M., in Damascus, Manṣūr  
  3. b. Isaac b. Saʿīd b. Pinhas gave the ring of security for Zuhra, the virgin, daughter of R. Jacob ha-Kohen, the scribe, according to
  4. the cus[tom of the] small [sy]nagogue of the Babylonians. They fixed  the mohar between them at seventy gold pieces: twenty-
  5. five, the mohar of virgins, and the rest as an addition for mohar. And he advanced at the time of this security
  6. fifteen good, fully weighed gold pieces. And at the time that he marries her, he shall pay her thirty-five,  
  7. the balance of the fifty. Rav Jacob, her father, accepted for her this ring of security, this [r]ing of security(!). He shall provide for
  8. her [needs] until the time of her marriage to her husband. Valid is this writ of betrothal in the laws (courts) of Israel. May they build and prosper.
  9. [Tw]enty delayed (gold pieces) are "on his neck." May he be built by her. Bishr [b.] Ismaʿīl  
  10. b. Ḥātim testified to this and to all that is in this writ.  
  11. Isaac b. Samuel b. Nihmī, witness.  
  12. Saʿīd b. 'Uzayr Ṭavyomi whose soul is at rest testified to all th[at] is in this.
  13. Elisha b. Moses ha-Kohen, witness.
  14. Samuel b. Solomon b. ʿUzayr, witness.  
  15. Ezra b. Samuel b. Ezra, witness. And Moses b. Samuel b. Ḥātim (who) r(ests in) E(den) testified.
  16. Nissin b. Savoy, witness.  
  17. David b. Aaron, witness. Abraham the judge ha-Levi berabbi.
