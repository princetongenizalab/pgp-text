English Translation of State document: Moss. IV,56.1

S. D. Goitein, "Prayers from the Geniza for Fatimid Caliphs, the Head of the Jerusalem Yeshiva, the Jewish Community and the Local Congregation," in Studies in Judaica, Karaitica and Islamica, Presented to Leon Nemoy on his Eightieth Birthday (Bar-Ilan University Press, 1982).

  1. (May) the prayer of God and His blessings,
  2. His ever-growing benefactions,
  3. His greetings and call of Peace (be) upon
  4. our lord and master, the Imām
  5. al-Ḥāfiẓ li-dīn Allāh,

margin

  1. the Commander of the Faithful.
