Verso
If Sirius rises on day 25 of Abīb
and the moon is lodged in Cancer, then it indicates
what will be in it (the year): gentle winds that will come
from the north. But this good will not last.
The Nile will rise high. There will appear among people evil
bile and an evil, malignant illness. An eclipse will be seen,
and a group of leaders and peers (?) will die.
There will be fighting discord among people, especially among
the notables. Wheat will be scarce and in demand. There will be
fighting in Egypt and Syria. There will be fear for the king
on account of illness and other causes of destruction.
If it rains water for 3 days, there will be no benefit,
and it will be a sign of the king's demise and the cessation of benefaction.
Many cattle will perish. People will be displaced from
the district of Sudan. The moon will be eclipsed in
the district of the east and toward the south until nothing
