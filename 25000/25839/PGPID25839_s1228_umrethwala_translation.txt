And he demanded from them a fine of a head of cattle, four of them, and he did not consider [?]
from it at all [any] khafāra. The servants ask for the issuance of the obeyed order
concerning the aforementioned matter, in accordance with justice, so that they should not be made to pay more than the recognised amount
for a head of cattle, which he stated was lacking, without increase or deduction [?],
and without injustice; and that his hand be restrained from oppressing them, as an act of justice toward them, God willing.
Praise be to God alone, and His blessings be upon our lord Muḥammad and his progeny.
God is sufficient for us, and He is the best disposer of affairs.
