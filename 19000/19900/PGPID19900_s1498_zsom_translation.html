English Translation of Legal document: T-S 10K20.8

Dora Zsom, "Estate Inventory (Fustāt, Egypt)," in Posen Library of Jewish Culture and Civilization (Yale University Press, 2026), vol. 3.

Translated by Dora Zsom.

The weekly Torah portion “Va-etḥanan” [Deuteronomy 3:23–7:11]; the entire Book of [Commandments by] Ḥefets [ben Yatsliaḥ]; [halakhic] questions; the Introduction [to the Talmud] of Samuel ben Ḥofni [Ga’on]; [Rashi’s] commentary on tractate Sukkah; [halakhic] questions; Gemara: Me‘ilah; commentary on Yevamot; Tractate Niddah; three booklets: a commentary on Niddah, a commentary on Bava Batra, and a commentary on Makkot; Book of Geometry in Hebrew letters; collected midrashim; Fasts; [Se‘adya Ga’on’s] Book of Refutation of the Separatists [i.e., the Karaites]; Gemara: Bava Meẓi’a; glossary of Seder Mo‘ed; subjects connected to the year [i.e., the daily prayers for the whole year]; commentary on Job; midrash of the sages [possibly chapter 6 of Pirke avot]; questions; commentary on Genesis; collection of midrash; [Nissim Ibn Shāhīn’s] The Scroll of Secrets; some of [the Muslim philosopher] al-Kindi’s treatises; questions; homiletic interpretation of the [book of] Lamentations; [Se‘adya Ga’on’s] Book of the Spheres [on astronomy]; the Twelve Prophets; Scripture; commentary on 

verso

Bava Batra; [Samuel ben Ḥofni Ga’on’s] Book [of Abutter’s Rights] to Preempt [Sale]; glossary of the Mishnah; questions; additional questions; midrash on Ḥayye Sarah [Genesis 23:1]; penitential prayers for the Day of Atonement; Samuel; Scripture; commentary on Job; homiletical sermons; questions; a bundle containing notes and reminders; [various] booklets; penitential prayers for the Day of Atonement; a bundle containing booklets; a booklet containing an index of responsa; Evel Rabbati [the minor tractate on mourning]; Book of Tradition; explication of words; a book on medicine; homiletic interpretation of the [book of] Ruth; questions; a bundle with penitential prayer; Hayya Ga’on’s Norms of Conduct for the Judge.

Isaac ben Samuel, may he rest in peace

Abraham bar Shemaiah he-ḥaver [the fellow], great-grandson of Shemaiah Ga’on

Abraham ben Aaron he-ḥaver

verso, right margin

A booklet was brought containing all the compositions of the geonim and someone among those who were present said that it was the property of Ezekiel he-ḥaver [ben Eli ha-Kohen].

verso, upper margin

And we have found the half part of [tractate] Ketubbot. [Written] on it is . . . Tsedakah ha-Kohen and the commentary on Bava Batra by Ibn al-Sulami.
