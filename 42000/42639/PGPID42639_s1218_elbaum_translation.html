English Translation of State document: T-S 20.172

Alan Elbaum, Unpublished Editions (n.p., 2026).

  1. [. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . the emissary of the ruler of the land]
  2. of al-Rūm, may his triumph be glorious.     Let our lord settle his business
  3. and conclude the affair with him in accordance with his noble promise
  4. and charge them with good faith in his dealings with them—before
  5. the arrival of the emissaries of our lord the sulṭān al-Malik al-ʿĀdil,
  6. may his triumph be glorious—and reassuring his heart. Let him (i.e., you) not give preference 
  7. to anyone over them, and let our lord, may his triumph be glorious, not heed the speech
  8. of the one who advised him as he did and who reported to him the matter
  9. of the Georgians and the ruler of Arzan al-Rūm, for to him
