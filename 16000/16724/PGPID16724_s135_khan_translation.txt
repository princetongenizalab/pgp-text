Registration mark on right
[Let it be registered on behalf of the office o]f supervision,
if God wills.
Registration mark on left
Let it be registered in the office of accounts,
if God wills.
[They have registered the amount of] two, a quarter and a sixth.
Praise be to God for his bounties.
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm for the year four-hundred and three,
under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful,
ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant.
Half of this: One, an eighth and two qīrāts (= a twelfth). Its amount: Two, a quarter and a sixth.
Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The beginning of Ḏū al-Ḥijja,
in the year four-hundred and three.
