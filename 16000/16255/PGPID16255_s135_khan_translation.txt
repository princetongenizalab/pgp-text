In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to the warehouse-keeper in the province of al-Fayyūm on behalf of ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb, the estate manager,
for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makīn al-Dawla
wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, with ʾAbū al-Sarī
Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant.
Half of this: One, a third and an eight. Its amount: Two, a half, a third and an eighth dinārs.
Written by Mīḵā'īl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The twenty-fifth of Ḏũ al-Ḥijja
in the year four-hundred and three.
