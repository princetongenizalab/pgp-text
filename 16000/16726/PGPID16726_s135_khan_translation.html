English Translation of State document: T-S AS 183.66

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

Registration mark on right

  1. Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
  2. if God wills.
  3. They have registered the amount of three, a half and a sixteenth.
  4. Praise be to God, with the praise of those who give thanks.

Registration mark on left

  1. Let it be registered in the office of accounts,
  2. if God wills.
  3. They have registered the amount of three, a half and a sixteenth.
  4. Praise be to God for his bounties.
  5. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  6. A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm 
  7. [for the year] four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk
  8. [Makīn al-Dawla] wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, 
  9. with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis [acting as accountant]. 
  10. [Half of this:] One, [a half and a quarter]. Its amount: Three, a half and a sixteenth.
  11. [Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd] al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The thirteenth of
  12. Jumādā II, in the year four-hundred and three.
