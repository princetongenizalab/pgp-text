Recto
Registration mark on right
Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
if God wills.
Registration mark on left
Let it be registered in the office of accounts, 
if God wills. 
They have registered the amount of a sixth and an eighth.
Praise be to God for his bounties. 
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to the [warehouse-keeper] in the province of
al-Fayyūm on behalf of ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb the estate-manager for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhã, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, 
ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant. 
Half of this: Two qirāts (= a twelfth) and a sixteenth. Its amount: A sixth and an eighth.
Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The third of Ḏū al-Ḥijja, in the year four-hundred and three.
