Recto
Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
if God wills.
They have registered the amount of two, a half, a quarter and a sixth. 
Praise be to God for his bounties.
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm
for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk
Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAli ibn Bahār,  
with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant. 
Half of this: One, a quarter and a sixth. Its amount: Two, a half, a quarter and a  sixth.
Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The middle of Ḏū al-Qaʿda
in the year four-hundred and three.
