English Translation of State document: T-S AS 181.225

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
  2. if God wills.
  3. They have registered the amount of two, a half, a quarter and a sixth. 
  4. Praise be to God for his bounties.
  5. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  6. A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm
  7. for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk
  8. Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAli ibn Bahār,  
  9. with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant. 
  10. Half of this: One, a quarter and a sixth. Its amount: Two, a half, a quarter and a  sixth.
  11. Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The middle of Ḏū al-Qaʿda
  12. in the year four-hundred and three.
