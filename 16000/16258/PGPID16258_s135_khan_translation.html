English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.40.142.1

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

Registration mark on left

  1. Let it be registered in the office of accounts,
  2. if God wills.
  3. They have registered the amount of two and a quarter.
  4. Praise be to God, with the praise of those who give thanks.
  5. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  6. A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm
  7. for the year four-hundred and two, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk
  8. Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, and (his) deputy,
  9. the trusted judge ʾAbū al-ʿAlī al-Ḥasan ibn Yaḥyā ibn Bahār, with the elder
  10. Sadīd al-Dawla ʾAbū al-Faraj ʿAbd al-Masīḥ ibn Qūrīl acting as accountant.
  11. Half of this: One and an eighth. Its amount: Two and a quarter.
  12. Written by Mīḵaʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The seventeenth of Ḏū al-Qaʿda,
  13. in the year four-hundred and two.

Registration mark on right

3. They have registered the amount of two and a quarter.

4. Praise be to God for his bounties.
