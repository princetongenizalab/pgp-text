for the year four-hundred and three and it was received in the auspicious. chamber of the treasury and was delivered to the glorious warehouse,
in the presence of the judge Faḵr al-Dawla ʾAbū al-Faḍl Jaʿfar ibn ʾIbrāhīm ibn Sulaymān, who was responsible for the safekeeping
and the recording of cash received.
