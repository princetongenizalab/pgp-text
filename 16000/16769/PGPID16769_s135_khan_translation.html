English Translation of State document: T-S AS 183.28

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Verso

  1. Dīwān al-ʾIshrāf (Office of supervision)
  2. Makhzūma
  3. submitted by Muḥammad b. ʿAlī, the representative of the (sugar) factory and the house of honey at the press of....
  4. for pressing sugar cane, in the kharājī year four hundred and ninety, what is pressed in
  5. the months of the kharājī year four-hundred and ninety-one for the period beginning on
  6. Monday, the twenty-sixth of Barmūdā, and ending on Saturday, the first of Bashans of the aforementioned year.
  7. I testified to what this makhzūma contains and what ...[      ]... and its amount,
  8. [    ] the aforementioned amounts of its fine sugar. Written by ʿAlī b, al-Ḥasan b. ʿAlī in Rajab of the year four hundred and ninety-five
