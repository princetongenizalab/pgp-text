English Translation of Legal document: T-S AS 182.278

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate,  
  2. The slaves report the death of Fāḍila [ ]  
  3. the Jew, on Wednesday, the twenty-eighth [ in the year]  
  4. six-hundred and twenty-[ ] and she left her aforementioned son [ ]  
  5. he is called Abū Dāʾūd, mentioned herein .. [ on]  
  6. the aforementioned date. Praise be to God, the eternal.
