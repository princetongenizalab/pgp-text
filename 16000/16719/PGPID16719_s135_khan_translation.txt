Registration mark on right
Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
if God wills.
They have registered the amount of three, a third, a quarter, a sixth and an eighth.
Praise be to God, with the praise of those who give thanks.
Registration mark on left
Let it be registered in the office of accounts,
if God wills.
They have registered the amount of three, a third, a sixth and an eighth.
Praise be to God for his bounties.
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb for the sum owed through his standing surety for the estates in the town of al-Fayyūm for the year four-hundred and three, 
under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā,
the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, with ʾAbū al-Sarī 
Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant. 
Half of this: One, a half, a quarter and two qīrāṭs (= a twelfth). Its amount: Three, a third, a sixth and an eighth. 
Written by Mīkāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masiḥ, the cashier. Valid. The fifth of the month of Ramaḍān 
in the year four-hundred and three.
