Recto
Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,
if God wills. 
They have registered the amount of two, a quarter and a sixth. They have registered the amount of two, a quarter and a sixth. 
Praise be to God, with the praise of those who give thanks. Praise be to God for his bounties.
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
A valid receipt has been granted to the warehouse-keeper in the province of al-Fayyūm on behalf of ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb
the estate-manager, for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly-guided judge,
Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makin al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhã, the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, 
with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as accountant. 
Half of this: One, an eighth, and two qīrāts (= twelfth). Its amount: Two, a quarter and a sixth.
Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The twenty-fourth of Muḥarram, 
in the year four-hundred and four.
