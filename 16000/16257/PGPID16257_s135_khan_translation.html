English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.39.395

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto

  1. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  2. The amount owed by Basṭiyya ibn Marqūra for the tax-farm
  3. of the fallow fertile lands of Dayqūf, in the province of al-Bahnasā, 
  4. this being administered as the property of the office of the noble lady
  5. of al-ʾĀmir in the Muḥawwil chamber, upon her be the most excellent peace, 
  6. for the karājī year five-hundred and fifty.
  7. Produce: two hundred irdabbs.
  8. Wheat: One hundred and thirty-three irdabbs. Barley:
  9. Sixty-six irdabbs.

Verso

  1. Mark
  2. The settling of accounts
  3. by Basṭiyya ibn Marqūra for what he owes
  4. in respect of the tax-farm of the fallow fertile lands in Dayqūf
  5. for the ḵarājī year five-hundred and fifty-five.
