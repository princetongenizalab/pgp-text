Recto
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
The amount owed by Basṭiyya ibn Marqūra for the tax-farm
of the fallow fertile lands of Dayqūf, in the province of al-Bahnasā, 
this being administered as the property of the office of the noble lady
of al-ʾĀmir in the Muḥawwil chamber, upon her be the most excellent peace, 
for the karājī year five-hundred and fifty.
Produce: two hundred irdabbs.
Wheat: One hundred and thirty-three irdabbs. Barley:
Sixty-six irdabbs.

Mark
The settling of accounts
by Basṭiyya ibn Marqūra for what he owes
in respect of the tax-farm of the fallow fertile lands in Dayqūf 
for the ḵarājī year five-hundred and fifty-five.
