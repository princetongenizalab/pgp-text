English Translation of Letter: T-S 12.303

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Recto

  1. The sl[ave ...] 
  2. In (your) n(ame) O Me(rciful) 
  3. The slave of your e[x]cellency our lord and Gaon Maṣliaḥ ha-Kohen, head of the
  4. Academy of the Pride (Gaon) of Jacob, may your glory be exalted and your greatness increase,
  5. and may He compensate you with a male child for the sake of His name, and fulfiill in him
  6. the verse "In place of your fathers will be your sons, you will app[oint them]
  7. Dignitaries throughout the land" (Psalm 45: 17), and may He grant him favor and [kindness]
  8. and mercy, fulfilling the saying, ("and you will) [find favor] and a[pprobation in the eyes] of
  9. God and man" (Proverbs 3:4). I inform your excellency and great[n]ess
  10. that I am a b[lind] woman...
  11. with a blind child [...]. They dem[anded from me]
  12. the p[oll ta]x and we two do not even have br[e]ad [...].
  13. I ask God and your kindness [to give me]
  14. something to help me and my child against the troubles of [the time].
  15. You will earn your reward and recompense. I have
  16. never imposed on your generosity, even though your kindness,
  17. which everyone seeks, is like the sea. Your slav[e]
  18. is known as the wife of Samu(e)l. And [p]e[a]ce from your s[lave].
