English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J22.27

Renée Levine Melammed, "Women Fending for Themselves in Geniza Society," in “An Inspired Man”: Studies in Judeo-Arabic Culture Dedicated to the Memory of Joshua Blau (Brill, 2024), 340–62.

  1. In your name, O Merciful.
  2. May God grant peace to our master and teacher Maṣliaḥ haKohen,
  3. Head of the yeshiva “The Pride of Jacob,” may his authority last forever.
  4. Concerning what I will inform your honor, namely, your maidservant has been married to him
  5. Fifteen years now, and never received from him a thing
  6. Absolutely (nothing), not even the entrance fee for the bathhouse. Until this very day
  7. That I am with him, he did not (even) provide (lit. dress) me with underclothing, and your maidservant
  8. Complains about him, the harm and beatings; he keeps saying to me:
  9. “Redeem yourself.” And for what he does to me, may God impose on him the delayed payment (punishment);
  10. He is supposed to pay me what is in my ketubba since I suffer from this
  11. Bad character and from this harm for fifteen years.
  12. I implore God and you because I am a captive—
  13. Redeem me!
