Account for the shroud: 
A tunic: 19 qīrāṭs. A robe of Damietta (linen)
and a robe of green silk: 2⅔ and ¼ dinars. 
A scarf: 10½ qīrāṭs. Trousers:
9 dirhems. A turban: 30 dirhems
A cloak: 1 dinar. The coffin: 1½ dinars.
Trestles: 9½ dinars. Agarwood, camphor, and rosewater: 2. The
sawyer: ½. The tailor: 3 (dirhams). The journey through the desert: 3½.  
Judges and cantors: 24. Washer (of the body): 7. Someone to carry
the trestles: 1½. Pallbearers: 10. Gravedigger: 15.
Remaining in the hand of his wife: 5. Builders: 12. Watchman: 1.
Someone to backfill (the grave): 1. Remainder: 2½.
