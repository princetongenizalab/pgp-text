English Translation of List or table: T-S J1.31

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

  1. Account for the shroud: 
  2. A tunic: 19 qīrāṭs. A robe of Damietta (linen)
  3. and a robe of green silk: 2⅔ and ¼ dinars. 
  4. A scarf: 10½ qīrāṭs. Trousers:
  5. 9 dirhems. A turban: 30 dirhems
  6. A cloak: 1 dinar. The coffin: 1½ dinars.
  7. Trestles: 9½ dinars. Agarwood, camphor, and rosewater: 2. The
  8. sawyer: ½. The tailor: 3 (dirhams). The journey through the desert: 3½.  
  9. Judges and cantors: 24. Washer (of the body): 7. Someone to carry
  10. the trestles: 1½. Pallbearers: 10. Gravedigger: 15.
  11. Remaining in the hand of his wife: 5. Builders: 12. Watchman: 1.
  12. Someone to backfill (the grave): 1. Remainder: 2½.
