English Translation of Legal document: CUL Add.3423

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Recto

  1. We the undersigned witnesses on this wr[it] state
  2. that we know the situation of Sulaymān b. Ḥasan, and that he is poor,
  3. with few possessions. We do not know if he possesses any real estate or cash. He owes
  4. Baqā ha-Levi b. Ṣāliḥ three and a half dinars,
  5. which the latter has claimed from him in a Muslim court. He is not able to pay the fee for the police guard.
  6. So they threw him into prison. He has asked us, the undersigned, to report
  7. what we know about his situation. This was on the same Monday
  8. the eighteenth of Sivan 1486 (June 9, 1175) in the city of Minyat Ziftā, so that it be
  9. a conveyance of right and a proof. Sheʾerith ha-Kohen b. Yakhin ha-Kohen (who) r(ests in) E(den);
  10. Ṣedaqa b. Hillel ha-Kohen (who) r(ests in) E(den); Berakhot b. Yakhin (who) r(ests in E(den); 
  11. Josiah b. David (who) r(ests in) E(den); Nadiv b. David
  12. (who) r(ests in) E(den)
