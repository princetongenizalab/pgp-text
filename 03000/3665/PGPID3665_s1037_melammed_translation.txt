Recto


Abual-Faḍl, son of Ghazal the Alexandrian, came


 And obligated himself with a qinyan of his own free will


And not under duress, (so) that he will pay his wife,


Mother of his children, every month 20


Dirhem, each week 5 dirhems and 2 portions


Of wheat of high quality and the cost of their grinding. And already


He obligated her to him with a qinyan so that she will pay


From these 20 dirhems whatever he owes, such as


For the poll tax, and the rent for the house and the expenses


Of her household. And she will not request (of him) anything, as


Long as he pays her this (sum) via the supervision of


The court. And he will not demand of her (earnings from)


Her handiwork or her spinning and not that which (even)


Has the smallest value. And they both agreed to this


Together. And we performed with him a complete qinyan


With an implement fit for doing this. And this was


On the first of the month of Tishrei 1133.


Nathan the Cohen, son of Solomon the Cohen, may his soul rest in paradise.


 
1. And the words of (she whose) name is Khayra were completed.
