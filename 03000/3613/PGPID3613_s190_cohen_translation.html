English Translation of Credit instrument or private receipt: T-S 6J3.10

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Verso

  1. May the elder A[bu]ʾl-ʿIzz pay the bearer
  2. [on]e dirhem because my need for it is 
  3. urgent. God knows the situation I am
  4. in, so do not impede the messenger, but send him
  5. the dirhem quickly. May He open to you his treasure
  6. and relieve you from (having to resort to) human beings. May your welfare increase, as willed by him who lov[e]s you.
  7. Solomon (may) h(eaven) w(atch over him) and i(ncrease his good fortune).
