English Translation of Letter: T-S 13J20.20

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. In the n(ame of the) Me(rciful)  
  2. "Say as follows: 'To life! Greetings to you,'" etc. (1 Samuel 25:6). 
  3. –8. May your [h(onor)] g(reatness and) h(oliness), (our) ma(ster) and t(eacher) Solomon ha-Kohen the precious elder have many blessings and imminent salvation, pity, and compassion and the fulfillment of every request, much luck, and all available blessings. May God bless you and grant life to your son and let you see his joyous occasion and wedding canopy; and (your) h(onor), g(reatness), and h(oliness) (our) ma(ster) and te(acher) Yeshuʿa the precious elder, may God open before you the gates of mercy and grant life to your son and let you see his joyous occasion and wedding canopy, Amen forever.

  9. I am dispatching this letter to the most illustrious elders,
  10. may God preserve them, to inform them that the bearer of this (letter) is a man who was
  11. healthy, working strenuously in order to "conceal" himself and his family, when
  12. Fate betrayed him and he became weak, such that anyone looking at him needs no explanation about
  13. his condition. In addition, debt and the poll tax caught up with him. Whoever assists him
  14. with something with which he can maintain his way of life, shall be deemed to have made an offering (to God). He is
  15. ashamed for this has never been his habit. Whoever
  16. does him a good turn shall be deemed to have done so for the sake of God, and the Creator
  17. will magnify his reward. And peace. In addition to this, your son Maʿānī
  18. and his children ask about Father, the elder Abuʾl-Rabīʿ Sulaymān,
  19. and Ibrāhīm, and want them to grace us by coming on the holiday,
  20. may God bring it to us and you for many years to come and let us see the
  21. joyous occasion of the sons of Ibrahīm in your lifetime. I want you to come
  22. and grace us in the joyous occasion we are making for Abuʾl-Khayr. All our
  23. lives we have come to you for the holidays. By God, O [my] brother
  24. Ibrahīm, come visit your daughter so that her heart may take pleasure
