English Translation of Letter: T-S 13J28.13

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. [. . .] the collection, and exert yourself in this so you receive
  2. it. We notified you also that your
  3. “house” (wife) and the “house” (wife) of the beadle the e(lder) Sulaymān
  4. are the ones who shall collect from the women and exert themselves
  5. in collecting. [Do] not e[x]clude us from
  6. your noble thoughts and [yo]ur prayers
  7. which will be accepted (by God) in this esteemed holiday period.
  8. God the ex(alted) in his mercy will not forget you
  9. nor will He turn [y]ou back disappointed [. . .]

right margin, in another hand

[. . .] the h(aver) R. Yosef b. Shemuʾel (of) b(lessed) m(emory), who stands in the breach presented himself before the most illustrious Amīr, th[e] Amīr [Sayf] al-Dīn [. . .] they redeemed the items held in partnership [. . .] Go[d . . .] victory and may He make your habitations prosper [. . .] and sustain you [. . .] and pure intention [. . .] which he did, shall do.

verso

Abraham b. [. . .].
