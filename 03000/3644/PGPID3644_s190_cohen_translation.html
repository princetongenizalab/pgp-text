English Translation of Letter: T-S 6J6.21

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. In the name [of the Compassionate]
  2. “Behold God is my salvation,” etc.
  3. The cantor Faraj Allah, (may the) R(ock) p(rotect you), shall take note [that] the
  4. bearer of this is the orphan boy Musa. Ask
  5. the e(lder) Yiṣḥaq [about him]. If they refuse to give [him she]lter,
  6. please send a messenger and let me know immediately, so that [I can do]
  7. what is necessary to them. Beware of [being disobedient].
  8. And may God, the ex(alted), be your succor.
