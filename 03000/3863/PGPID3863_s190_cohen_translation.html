English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J41.1

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Recto

  1. [...k]isses the ground and informs the most illustrious
  2. [...] “the Precious Chosen One,” (may your) e(nd be) g(ood),
  3. the beneficent, who acts kindly toward his slave and [his] servant[t]
  4. at all times. Your slave is grateful for
  5. [...] for [...f]or your beneficence and for [...].
  6. May God the e[xal]ted make constant your high rank
  7. give you a peaceful end // and a loyal house // and grant that you live to see the redeemer,
  8. you and the whole house of Israel.
  9. Your slave is [experiencing] adversity on account of his being naked and ill
  10. and lacking food during the upcoming holiday.
  11. I have not even one fals (a copper coin of low value) with which to buy
  12. anything. I have earned nothing this year.
  13. Your charitable deeds toward your slave are many.
  14. Do not turn your slave away disappointed.
  15. And peace.
