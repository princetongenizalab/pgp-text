(Partial translation of two strophes)
Behold, ships, behold, ships coming into the port.
Go and see what merchandise they bring.
Beautiful girls.
Go and see what they were sold for.
For a barrel of straw.
Ah, captain, you have been paid too much.
Behold, ships, behold ships, coming into the port.
Go and see what merchandise they bring.
Handsome boys, finer than gold.
Go and see what they were sold for.
For a barrel of gold and gems.
Ah, captain, you have been paid too little.
