They said to him: “And why  did you deal with these things, 
and you teach an evil teaching to all creatures?
And these books that are in your hand—if you tell 
us the truth, whose (books) they are; and if not, we will kill
you, and t[hro]w your body to the animals of the field.”
He said to them: “These writings are not old
and they are not new, and they are not by Balaam.”
They said to him: “And from where do you have them?” He said: “They are by Yoḥanan
