English Translation of Letter: ENA NS 77.36

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. ... [Yiṣḥa]q the gr[eat] Rav, "the [He]lp of the Nesiut," "Head [of the Scholar]s"
  2. may his diadem blossom, son of Sam[u]el the pious the Sefaradi (Spaniard) of (blessed) m(emory)
  3. [... tha]t the bearer of this letter is a poor, down-and-out man who was [...].
