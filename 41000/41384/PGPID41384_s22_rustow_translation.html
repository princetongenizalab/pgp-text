Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of State document: St. Catherine, Atiya no. 8, Stern doc. 5

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. and likewise the grain and barley and [...]

  2. and the fruit at the Upper Monastery belonging to the bishop and the monks.

  3. There has been issued the order of the servant of our master and lord

  4. the imām al-Ḥāfiẓ li-Dīn Allāh, Commander of the Faithful,

  5. may God's blessings be upon him and his pure ancestors

  6. and most noble sons, the most illustrious lord, Commander of the Armies,

  7. Sword of Islam, Champion of the Imām, Succor of Mankind,

  8. Abū l-Muẓaffar Bahrām al-Ḥāfiẓī,

  9. to write this decree and to include therein (the stipulation) that orders and decrees

  10. have continually been issued protecting (the Monastery of) Sinai

  11. and the bishop belonging to it and its monks,

  12. and extending care over them, warding off harm from them,

  13. guarding and safeguarding them; and that when there was presented

  14. their petition containing their complaint, he censured its perpetrator.

  15. The order has been issued to the current military governor,

  16. to every governor who administers the districts

  17. and subdivisions in it (?), and to the officials, preventing them

  18. from interfering with anything belonging to them, whether wheat, barley,

  19. grains, palm trees and their fruit, vineyards and their fruit, and what is pressed from them,

  20. and what belongs to them by way of the supplies they procure

  21. for the purpose of assisting those who visit them, according to their custom in that matter

  22. and their established practice, or seeking anything from them by

  23. way of a loan or something else. Whoever encroaches

  24. upon that after this condemnation shall receive such reproof

  25. as will cause him grievous harm and serve as a deterrent to others.

  26. Whoever reads this or has it read to him from among all

  27. the governing amīrs, may God keep them safe and preserve them,

  28. and the officials, let him take notice

  29. of this command and act upon it and beware of transgressing it

  30. or overstepping it. Let it be deposited in the hand of its bearer after being delivered

  31. to its repository, God willing. Written

  32. on the third of Rajab of the year five hundred twenty-nine.

  33. Praise be to God alone, and God bless our lord Muḥammad his prophet and his family, the pure imāms, and grant them abundant peace.

  34. God is sufficient for us and an excellent guardian.

Registration mark between lines 30 and 31, right 

  1. It has been copied. Praise be to God, and in him I seek assistance.

Registration mark between lines 30 and 31, left 

  1. It has been copied in the Bureau of Supervision over the Frontier Ports,
  2. may they be protected, and over Noble Sinai.
  3. Praise be to God alone.

Registration mark between lines 31 and 32

  1. It has been copied in the Bureau of Supervision over the Eastern Districts.
