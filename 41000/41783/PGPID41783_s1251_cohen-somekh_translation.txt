The scholars (ʿulamāʾ) and learned men (fuqahāʾ) of every nation

with unquestionable proofs and clear-cut signs.

intellects have confirmed them, with the result that men of understanding prescribed

was confirmed by his [God's] unambiguous and truthful book in those
previously mentioned verses, as well as by others found
in the Scriptures. Therefore,
