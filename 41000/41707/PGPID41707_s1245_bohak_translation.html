Folio 4v (first section)

  1. This is the book of the sentencing that was decreed upon Yeshuʿa son of Pandera. And it was in the year
  2. two of Tiberinus Caesar, the king of Edom (i.e., Rome), they decreed a trial upon
  3. Yeshuʿa son of Pandera who rebelled against the God of Heaven, for he misled
  4. the people of the world with deeds of magic. And he was from the town
  5. Nazareth in the land of the town Rome, which this thing happened in the town
  6. of the Jews. On this day, on the twenty-eighth of the month of Elul,
  7. Petalus the governor approached and came into the market, and summoned Yehudah the orchard-man,
  8. and he took him before Yehoshuʿa ben Peraḥiah the Nasi, and he brought him
  9. before Marinus the elder of the Jews and in front of crowds of people.
  10. And they asked this Yehudah, and they said to him: “A certain man whose name is Yeshuʿa, and he
  11. is misleading the people of (the) world with a deed of magic—do you know him?”
  12. And he said to them: “Yes, I know him (and) that he is misleading people, and he
  13. is in magic, and also misleads the people of the world. And we discovered in his hand this deed,
  14. and he chose a place there, and he is misleading the people of the world in a certain town.
  15. Now, if my master wishes I will go and bring him and deliver him to you.”
  16. Yehudah the orchard-man went and found him with many people sitting in front of him,
  17. and he was saying to them that “I will heal you.” And he said to them:
  18. “I am God.” And they were bowing down to him. And Yehudah the orchard-man
  19. caught him and came with him before them. They asked Yeshuʿa the wicked, and they said to him:
  20. “From which nation/place (?) are you?” And he said to them: “I am a Jew, and I am also a mamzer.”
  21. “And your father too—what was his name, and what was he doing?” He said to them: “My father’s name is Pandera,
  22. and he is a Gentile in Israel, and he is a man who plays.” They said to him: “What is the name 
  23. of your mother and what are her doings?” He said to them: “Her name is Miriam, and she plaits the 
  24. hair of women (into) plaits.” They also said to him: “What is the name of your mother’s husband, and what
  25. is his profession?” He said to them: “His name is Yoseph, and he is a carpenter.”
  26. They also said to him: “What is your name and what are your doings?” He said: “Me—my name is Yeshuʿa, and I am a
  27. hunter;  I hunt the fish.” And they said to him: “You are empty (?) of all friend (?). Why
  28. did you not chase (?) empty (?) the birds? (?).” They also said to him: “The books that are
  29. in your hand— are they old or new?” He said to them: “They are old,
