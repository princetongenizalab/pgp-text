English Translation of Letter: ENA 2738.37

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Recto

  1. Your slave Yaʿqūb 
  2. b. Abu ʾl-Yumn 
  3. ln (your name, O Mercifu)l
  4. "Happy is he who is thoughtful of the wretched; in bad times may the Lord keep him (from harm)" (Psalm 41:2). 
  5. God the ex(alted) knows the state the slave of your excellency
  6. the nagid, may your rule be established forever, is in. I inform
  7. your highness about my situation, my poverty and indigence,
  8. my desolation and illness. I ask you
  9. kindly, may God perpetuate your strength, to help me 
  10. during this noble holiday with something with which I may celebrate.
  11. Your slave, were he no[t] confined to
  12. the house, unable to go out on account of the poll tax
  13. and had he not been sick for eight months,
  14. would have been able to earn a living.
  15. Help would free me from complaining. You are suited to do this.
  16. May the holy one, blessed be He, double your reward and be your succor
  17. and grant you a (good) e[nd] and hope. And may your excellency's welfare
  18. increase and not diminish. And so may it be His will.
