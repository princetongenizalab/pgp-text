English Translation of Letter: ENA 1822a.60

S. D. Goitein, A Mediterranean society; the Jewish communities of the Arab world as portrayed in the documents of the Cairo Geniza (University of California Press, 1967-1993, 1967), vol. 3.

Recto

  1. The servant of her favor
  2. Bū Saʿd b. al-Dayyān
  3. In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate.
  4. My lady, Sitt Ṣalaf visited me and reported to me what you have instructed her.
  5. Now, my lady, I have children; so if you deem fit that he/it should be left in my possession, (it is fine with me);
  6. but if you transfer him/it for me to someone else, you would do me a great favor.
  7. I am also trustworthy in this matter like two
  8. legally fit witnesses. So, if you will do this
  9. for me, your action will be highly appreciated. Farewell.
