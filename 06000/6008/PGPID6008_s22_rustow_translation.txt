— and returned to him: 1 dīnār. Weighed: 1 dīnār less a qīrāṭ.
Bahabshīm for 4 loads
of the first 200, at a rate of 12 and one-half and one-quarter —

For 3 loads and one-half and 11 raṭls

Idrīja for 4 loads and one-half:
expenses (mūna): 10 dāniqs per load: 45 dinars, weighed.
From it, for 8 loads and one-half and 86
raṭls at a rate of 12 [qīrāṭs] per load; price: 104 dīnārs, weighed.
