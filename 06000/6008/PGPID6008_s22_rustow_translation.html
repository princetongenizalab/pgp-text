Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of List or table: T-S Ar.51.87

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto, right column

  1. In the name of God the [compassionate, the merciful.]
  2. Details of the purchases of my lord and mast [er … ]
  3. b. Ibrāhīm b. ʿAllān, may God perpetuate his glory.
  4. ʿAbdallāh al-Būṣīrī: 40 raṭls, gross (wasaṭ), price: 1 dīnār less a qīrāṭ.
  5. Sons of ʿAbd al-Ṣamad: 38 raṭl, fine (naqī), price: 1 dīnār less a qīrāṭ; ʿAlī:
  6. 133 [raṭls], fine; price: 3 [dīnārs] and one-quarter. Weight of Naṣrūn:
  7. sent, minus 5 raṭls; gross; price: 2 dīnārs. Weight of
  8. the qāḍī: 134 [raṭls], gross, remainder 25; price: 4 dāniqs less one-sixth.
  9. The brokers (al-samāsira): 26 raṭls, gross; price: 1 dīnār, weighed.
  10. Idrīs: 100 raṭls, fine; price: 5 dāniqs, weighed.
  11. Abū ʿAlī: 100 raṭls, fine; price: 2 dīnārs and one-quarter, counted.
  12. Idrīs: 125 [raṭls], gross; price: 3 [dīnārs] less 2 qīrāṭs and one-half, weighed.
  13. Farhās: 86 [raṭls], gross; price: 1 dīnār and two-thirds, weighed.
  14. al-Khaṭīb: 155 raṭls, fine; price: 7 dāniqs and one-third, weighed.
  15. Idrīs: 144 [raṭls], gross; price: 3 [dīnārs] and one-third, weighed.
  16. The brokers — for madder plants: 146 [raṭls]; price: 3 [dīnārs] and one-half and an one-eighth, weighed.
  17. al-Idrījī: 20 raṭls; price: ½ dinar,
  18. — and returned to him: 1 dīnār. Weighed: 1 dīnār less a qīrāṭ.
  19. Bahabshīm for 4 loads
  20. of the first 200, at a rate of 12 and one-half and one-quarter —
  21. what is added to our account: 53 dīnārs and one-half.
  22. For 3 loads and one-half and 11 raṭls
  23. fine, at a rate of 11 and one-half: 40 dīnārs and one-half, weighed.
  24. And from Bahabshīm for gold (ʿayn = gold coin) received,
  25. 320 raṭl, gross; price: 8 dāniqs, weighed.
  26. Idrīja for 4 loads and one-half:
  27. expenses (mūna): 10 dāniqs per load: 45 dinars, weighed.
  28. From it, for 8 loads and one-half and 86
  29. raṭls at a rate of 12 [qīrāṭs] per load; price: 104 dīnārs, weighed.
  30. Half of what was held up in Idrīja: 5 dāniqs and a half and a quarter and 2 qīrāṭs.

Recto, left column

  1. [… raṭl and one quarter …]
  2. Total of all that: 338 dīnārs and 2 qīrāṭs.
  3. ⟦Farhās: 107 raṭl — 5 dāniqs less a sixth⟧ Idrīs: 2 dīnārs.
  4. All [the goods] were baled into 34 bales, and owed for them:
  5. Carrying the flax via Bahabshīm and hiring beasts: 53 dirhams less one quarter.
  6. Hiring beasts to Dilāṣ, and bringing in the flax
  7. via Bahabshīm, and a gratuity to the carters — his share: 22½ dirhams.
  8. Carrying the flax from Idrīja, bringing it in, and a gratuity
  9. to the carters: 30½ dirhams.
  10. Expenses of the packers, storage fee (ḥizāmayn → kufra), market fee (sūq), carrying,
  11. and bribe (barṭīl) for the first 12: 35 dirhams.
  12. Expenses of the packers, storage fee, market fee, carrying,
  13. and bribe for the other 17: 65 dirhams.
  14. Expenses of the packers, storage fee, market fee, carrying,
  15. and bribe for the 5 of the third portion: 16 dirhams.
  16. Bribe for the whole: two-thirds and a qīrāṭ.
  17. Baling the 4 bales: 2 dīnārs and one-half.
  18. Price of the inner packing cloth and dispatch documents for 34 [bales]: 5 dāniqs less one-eighth.
  19. Outer packing cloth for 34 bales: 4 dāniqs less one quarter.
  20. String (khayṭ) for them: 34 dirhams.
  21. Rope (ḥabl) for them: ½ and ¼ and ⅛ dīnār.
  22. Scribe (kātib): 17 dirhams.
  23. Brokerage (samsara): 6 dāniqs and two-thirds.
  24. Boat hire (kirāʾ markab) for 29 [units]: 3 dāniqs and 5 qīrāṭs.
  25. Total gold (ʿayn): 22 dīnārs and two-thirds and a qīrāṭ,
  26. Silver (waraq): 373¼ [dirhams],
  27. at an exchange rate of 35: 8 dāniqs and 2 qīrāṭs.
  28. Total gold and silver: 30 dīnārs, two-thirds, and one-eighth.
  29. Total amount for the 34 bales: 369 dīnārs less one-eighth.

Verso

  1. [… two minas …]
  2. For the oil (?): 310 dinars.
  3. Total: 422 dinars less a sixth.
  4. Remainder owed to him: 53 dinars less a qīrāṭ.
  5. Farhās: 5 less a sixth. Idrīs: 2 dinars.
  6. Remainder owed to him: 51 [dinars] less a qīrāṭ.
  7. Received in Fusṭāṭ: 14 dinars and a half; remainder of expenses: ½ and ⅛ dinar less a ḥabba.
  8. Remainder owed to him: 37 dinars less a sixth less a ḥabba.
  9. He has to his credit in Fusṭāṭ a purse of dinars (ṣurra); and he has to his credit an order of payment (ruqʿa): 3 [dinars] and a qīrāṭ,
  10. and he has to his credit from the purse: 1 dinar and a half and a third and a ḥabba; total: 15 dinars less a qīrāṭ and a ḥabba.
  11. Owed by him to Dār al-Kattān for 5 bales: 10 dāniqs and an eighth.
  12. And owed by him to Dār Mānak for 3 barqalū [items],
  13. Rashīd and al-Mukhtāra: 2 dinars and ½ qīrāṭ.
  14. To the clerks (ṣibyān) of al-Mukhtāra: 3 and a half, and waraq;
  15. gratuity: 6 dirhams.
  16. Boat hire to Rashīd: ¾ dinar less a ḥabba.
  17. Carrying and a dispatch letter and a ¼ qīrāṭ — and for them also: 2 qīrāṭs
  18. and a ḥabba; and binding and delivery: 20 dirhams.
  19. Boat hire at Būṣīr for the 5th [batch]: two-thirds less a dāniq.
  20. Total gold: 17½ and an eighth.
  21. Waraq: [… and a half], which in gold: 20 qīrāṭs and a ḥabba.
  22. Total gold and waraq: 14 dinars and a third and an eighth and a ḥabba.
  23. Remainder owed to him: a third and an eighth.
