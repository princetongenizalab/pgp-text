English Translation of Legal document: ENA 4010.7 + ENA NS 19.14

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, "The Ransom-Divorce: Divorce Proceedings Initiated by the Wife in Mediaeval Jewish Practice.," Israel Oriental Studies 6 (n.p., 1976), 288-307.

[Translation of all three fragments together]

  1. He who will affix his signature at the bottom of these lines declares: We were present during quarrels which took place between Abū ʿAli 
  2. (our) master) Japheth b. (our) master) Manasseh r(est in) E(den) and his wife Milāḥ b. Xayrūn r(est in) E(den). He said to her, "Word has reached me that Barakat b. 
  3. Abu Saʿid the perfumer has enticed you away (from me). And you want to divorce me to marry him." Then she said, "I would marry 
  4. that one?!" She swore by great oaths that she would never marry him. Then we came with this Abu ʿAli 
  5. as he met with this Barakāt b. Abū Saʿīd. Abū ʿAlī said to him, “You rascal! 
  6. You scoundrel! You entice my wife away (from me) and tell her 'Divorce him so that I can marry you'." And there were m[any?] words between them 
  7. and argument, blaming and attacks, some in earnest and some in jest. Then said this Barakāt b. [Abū Ṣaʿid,]
  8. [t]he perfumer... [... o]f the undersigned, of our fellows, "Serve as my witnesses in wh[at I shall say]
  9. to you. I bind myself with powerful oaths that were I to m[arry...]
  10. I undertake, as of now, to pay to the poor of Egypt fifty dinars [...] 
  11. to her, and I have [no]t asked her to marry nor anything else [..."] 
  12. this Japheth Abū ʿAli [...] to this. They reconciled and par[ted...] 
  13. "Sign for me your signatures to what Barakā[t] has said [.....”]
  14. with his free will and his vol[ition]. And because it was permissible for us to accept [this testimony... We wrote] 
  15. for him these lines and signed our testimony below [... We wrote]
  16. and signed and gave to this m(aster) Japheth b. m(aster) [Manasseh...] 
  17. for a deed of right and evidence. Our signing was delayed until [...]
  18. year 1406 of the era [by which we are accustomed to count...]
  19. Which is situated on the river Nile. Strong and firm [...] 
  20. Benjamin b. Abraham
