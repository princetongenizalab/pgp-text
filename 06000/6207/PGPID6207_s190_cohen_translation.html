English Translation of Legal document: BL OR 5542.3

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. We witnesses whose signatures are placed at the end of this document
  2. state that we know and attest that the elder Abuʾl-Fakhr the perfumer, who is known as Ibn
  3. al-Amshāṭī, (our ) ma(ster) and t(eacher) Saʿadya the elder son of (our) m(aster) and t(eacher) Avraham the precious elder (who) r(ests in) E(den)
  4. served as collector of the mezonot during the middle third of the month of Kislev of the year 1473
  5. of (the Era of) Contracts in Fustat, Egypt,
  6. which sits on the Nile River, under the a[uthority] of his excellency, the precious,
  7. the garland, the splendor, his honor, greatnes[s], and hol[iness] our (master) and teacher, the uniqu[e] in his generation,
  8. our prince, prince of God in our midst, our lord Nethanel ha-Levi,
  9. “Israel’s chariots and horsemen” (2 Kings 2:12), may his name live forever. Accordingly we wrote our signatures on the
  10. abovementioned date. All confirmed. Shelomo ha-Kohen b. Yehuda ha-Kohen of b(lessed) m(emory)
  11. Tiqva b. Shemarya b. Mevasser
  12. the ḥaver (who) r(ests in) E(den)
