English Translation of List or table: T-S 8J17.18

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

P.1, recto

  1. Account of what was collected from the pledge drive
  2. for the captives, may God release them,
  3. in the Rīf.

P.1, verso

  1. In (your) name, O Merc(iful)
  2. Amount collected from the Rīf in the pledge drive for the captives,
  3. may God release them, that was ordered by his excellency,
  4. honor, greatness, holiness, the wreath, the glory, our master and teacher,
  5. our lord Sa[m]uel the Great Nagid, Chief of the Dignitaries, Nagid of the
  6. Nagids, Master of Masters, Right-hand of the Government, Diadem of the Nesiut,
  7. may his glory be exalted and his greatness increase, whose collection was in the charge of
  8. his servant Abuʾl-Surūr b. Ṭarīf, (who) r(ests in) E(den), and the elder Abuʾ
  9. l-Maʿālī, (his) ho(nor) o(ur) ma(ster) and te(acher) Shemuʾel the precious elder, son of o(ur) te(acher)
  10. Yehudah the honored elder, (who) r(ests in) E(den)
  11.           in gold and waraq (silver coins) at the exchange rate of 40
  12. –13.   dirhems to the dinar—225 3/8 dinars.
  14. Itemization: al-Maḥalla      40 1/4 dinars
  15. Minyat Ziftā                         37 dinars

P. 2, recto

  1. Sambuṭiya                     26 dinars
  2. Damsīs                           12 dinars
  3. Sammanūd                    11 dinars
  4. Malīj                                28 dinars
  5. Damīra                            14 dinars
  6. Tinnīs                               3 dinars
  7. the Elder Abū Saʿd
  8. and the Elder Abuʾl-Maʿālī    20 dinars
  9. Benhā al-ʿAsl                 14 1/8 dinars
  10. Damietta                         20 dinars
  11. –12. Total: 225 3/8 dinars
  12. Transferred by the Elder Abuʾl-Maʿālī 
  13. to Fustat to the trust of the Elder Abū
  14. Saʿd — 37 dinars

P. 2, verso

  1. and also from him what he and his partner the
  2. elder Abū Saʿd, (may the) Mer(ciful) wa(tch over him), were in charge of, to the elder Abuʾl-Munā
  3.        in gold coin—20 dinars
  4. Collected by the captives as per his request, with the letter
  5. of his excellency the Nagid, may his rule continue forever
  6.        in gold coin—93 dinars
  7. Remaining with the Elder Abū Manṣūr “The Designate” in Damietta,
  8. (may the) Mer(ciful) wa(tch over him)        in gold coin—20 dinars
  9. Transferred by Abuʾl-Surūr b. Ṭarīf, (who) r(ests in) E(den), to
  10. the trust of the Elder Abū Saʿd—55 dinars.
  11. Transferred by Muʿammar, “The Treasure of his Community,” to the seat of our lord, may he live forever, paid to the captives from Rum,
  12. in waraq valued at 45 dirhems (per dinar): one dinar and 1/8.
  13. Grand total—226 1/8 dinars.
