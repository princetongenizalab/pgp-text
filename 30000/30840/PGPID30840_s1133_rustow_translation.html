English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.35.48

Marina Rustow, unpublished translations.

Verso

  1. [God’s prayers be upon him, his pure ancestors and] 
  2. expected [sons]. To him belong the answer to this and to other things, and the lofty opinion
  3. on what he has asked, in his worthiness, if God, may he be exalted, wills. Praise be to God alone, and God’s prayers on our lord (Muḥammad) and his family, and utmost peace.
