Translated by Dora Zsom
Yosef ibn Sulaymān, known as the dayyan [judge], and also Sahl ibn Mūsā, the cantor, appeared before me and acknowledged that the agreement between Khulayf ibn Yeshu‘a ben Nārūz and the daughter of Nuḥaym ibn Maymūn was on condition . . . and she is permitted to the one who will take the dowry of his daughter . [ . . . ]
Thus wrote Muḥammad ibn Ibrāhīm ibn Muḥammad ibn Ḥamdūn in the month of Rabi’ al-Awwal, in the [Muslim] year 434.
