Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: ENA 3793.2 + T-S NS J402

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. In (your n)ame, Merci(ful One).
  2. The letter of his excellency, the most illustrio[us] elder, has arrived.
  3. [It was] the most gladdening [letter that has come] and the most delightf[ul] missive to arrive. I read it with joy, pondered it [happy and pleased], and understood it.
  4. News of his well-being and good condition gladdened me, and I praised G[od, e]xalted be He, grea[tly] for it.
  5. I asked Him to grant [him] more of every good,
  6. by His mercy, God willing. My lord the most illustrious elder has described no longing that your excellency’s servant does not feel many times over.
  7. [I ask] God [to make] his honor [last] and to ease a reunion between me and him in the
  8. happiest circumstances and with the most contented mind, by His grace and generosity, God willing. My lord mentioned
  9. that he was held up this year for various reasons, and also because of the high price of cloth (bazz). Perhaps it is for the best; may God,
  10. exalted be He, choose what is best for him. No doubt the chief cause of the delay was none other
  11. than Shaykh Isḥāq al-Nafūsī. As for the cloth, had he bought it at any price, however high,
  12. it would have been a great profit. This year in Aden the bleached (maqṣūr) [cloth]
  13. sold at a market price of two dinars … the unbleached (khām) …
  14. 22 dinars per bale (khurja). There was a tremendous demand for cloth. He mentioned [...]
  15. and it was slack (kāsid), and that he was upset about it. My lord the [most illustrious] elder,
  16. may God not deprive me of your kindness, do not take it to heart. Let it be sold for a dirham or anything else, and kindly
  17. buy with the proceeds a small quantity of musk pods (nāfija) or good loose musk (misk nathr). As for the khumāsiyyāt (“fives”),
  18. my lord should not buy any of them, either for himself or for anyone else. They are worth nothing in
  19. Fustat, or in Aden either. The bale of maḥābis that my lords kindly
  20. bought has arrived. May God not deprive my lord of beneficence, as he has always been. By God, my lord,
  21. we missed you this year,  and his delay was hard on his servant. But there was
  22. nothing my lord could do. May God choose what is best for him and bring it to a good
  23. end. My most illustrious lord mentioned the small item of his held by the elder Abū Saʿīd
  24. b. Maḥfūẓ, and that the elder Khalaf  b. Yiṣḥaq should take delivery of it. This year the elder 
  25. Abū Saʿīd arrived from India after a good journey. He handed over what belonged to the excellency of my lord
  26. to the elder Khalaf b. Yiṣḥaq, and it passed into his possession. Pepper had a good market: we sold each
  27. bahār of pepper for thirty-eight dinars and a little better. As for the elder Khalaf,
  28. your servant does not know at what price he sold his excellency’s goods. Also, what belonged to his excellency of the cloth arrived with the elder Barakāt
  29. al-Ḥalabī, as my lord described in his letter,
