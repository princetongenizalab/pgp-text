English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J9.15

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. In the name of the Compassionate
  2. “Behold, God is my salvation,” etc.
  3. The noble community
  4. in Fustat, may their Rock bless them, a(men) f(orever) s(ela), knows that
  5. the duty of charity brings a great reward in His,
  6. the ex(alted’s), pr[esence]. This is especially so when the person is poor and embarrassed and old,
  7. such as the bearer, Yeshuʿa, (may he have a) g(ood) e(nd).
  8. It is in your beneficent nature to help
  9. and assist him because he wishes to travel
  10. to his city, Jerusalem, the Holy City, may it be rebuilt and est(ablished),
  11. for his children are waiting for him. Therefore, take up a collection
  12. for him in the two congregations and open your hands wide
  13. in giving to him, for this is the time for virtuous generosity. “Let not
  14. the downtrodden turn away disappointed” (Psalm 74:21). May God the ex(alted) be your
  15. succor, make your habitations prosper, grant long life to your children,
  16. and increase your sustenance. And peace.
