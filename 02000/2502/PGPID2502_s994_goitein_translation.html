English Translation of Legal document: Bodl. MS heb. f 56/45 + Bodl. MS heb. f 56/46

S. D. Goitein, "The Moses Maimonides-Ibn Sana Al-Mulk Circle: A Deathbed Declaration from March 1182," Moshe Sharon, ed., Studies in Islamic History and Civilization in Honour of Professor David Ayalon (Brill, 1986), 399 - 405.

Recto

  1. On Wednesday, the eighteenth of the month of Nisan 1493
  2. we were present with the sheikh Abū ʿl-Faraj, the esteemed notable, the wise and prudent, son of
  3. (his) h(onor), (the) g(reat) and h(oly) m(aster) and t(eacher) Moses, the esteemed elder, known as Ibn al-Kallām, (may he) r(est in) E(den), and found him
  4. being ill, but his mind present. He said to us: Please take notice that I owe to our lord,
  5. our master and teacher Moses (may his) Rock) k(eep him), 2 ½ Egyptian dinars; also to him
  6. another sum, on the claim of which he is trustworthy. To the sheikh al-Muwaffaq, (may his) Rock) k(eep him),
  7. 4 ⅙ dinars for the poll tax, which he paid for me;
  8. Also to him the price of wheat, for the price of which he is completely trustworthy up to 10 dinars.
  9. To the illustrious Qāḍī Ibn Sanāʾ al-Mulk 1 dinar and
  10. 12 dirhams. To the illustrious faqīh Ibn Ṣawla 4 dinars.
  11. To Abū ʾI-Khayr of Haifa 5 dirhams. I am giving to my wife
  12. called ... the daughter of my maternal uncle, the Sheikh Abū Saʿd \\ (may he) r(est in) E(den) // the entire third
  13. of the entire house in the al-Mamṣūṣa quarter in Fusṭāṭ-Miṣr
  14. and grant her complete proprietorship over it on condition that she does not marry again after my death.
  15. However, if she marries \\ after my death //, she will receive only 10 dinars of Egyptian currency
  16. promised here as the late installment of (my gift noted in) her marriage contract. He passed away
  17. from this illness to his eternal abode. We have written and
  18. signed that which has occurred in our presence so that it may serve as a title of right and a proof. Shemu’el ha-Levi b. Saʿadya, (may he) r(est in) E(den)
  19. Elʿazar b. Micha’el, (may he) r(est in) E(den)
