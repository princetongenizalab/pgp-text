Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: CUL Or.1080 J21

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. I am writing to you, my daughter, from Jerusalem, may God keep it, with three (days) remaining in the month of Rajab. What I have to tell you,
  2. my daughter, is what preoccupies me about your affairs. A fire [burns] in my heart because of you. God stands between me and the one who oppressed you
  3. and made you an orphan in my lifetime. You, my daughter, must depend on people’s kindness because of the deeds
  4. of your mother. I beseech God the exalted not to forgive her the sin by which, because of her greed, she destroyed you. God will set you right
  5. from her account. Know, my daughter, that I have sworn a solemn oath not to send you anything
  6. as long as I am uncertain about your status and do not know what to do about your situation. God knows that I have no
  7. consternation or worry other than for you. Rather, my health and my affairs are all in a state of benefaction,
  8. and my clothes do not hold me for happiness and good fortune. Had I sought nothing but worldly gain, then I could
  9. regard myself today as a great success, for I have become the administrator of a government compound in al-Shām for an ample
  10. salary and a good . . . [income?]. Your maternal aunt has seen me, and her husband has too, and also Abu l-Faḍl.
  11. At my disposal are men, commandment and prohibition, and I am powerful. God has made me better than I had been
  12. before. So good fortune has been mine and misfortune your mother’s, God be praised. Now, <my daughter>, I do not know with
  13. whom you are. I do not know whether you are with the Jews, who are the stock of your father, or the stock of your mother,
  14. the non-Jews. But this I wish you to know: even if they wanted to sell you to me, my own daughter, I would buy you and rescue you
  15. from their hands. What else could I do? I say that after Shavuʿot (ʿAnṣara), I will set out
  16. for the lands of the Rūm (Byzantium), my country and my family. Let me know first what your intentions are so that I can decide what to do,
  17. God willing.
