English Translation of Legal document: Yevr.-Arab. I 1701

Oded Zinger, "Between the Muslim state and individual agency: the regulation of sexuality in the Jewish communities of Medieval Egypt," in A Companion to Crime and Deviance in the Middle Ages (Cambridge University Press, 2023), 376–396.

  1. Bad rumours about
  2. Abū Naṣr ben Qayyūma multiplied for
  3. several years regarding the daughter of Abū al- Maʿālī b. Tāmār, who is the
  4. wife of Abū Saʿd b. Ṣedaqa the milkman. He carried out with her obscenities and flirtations. He exceeded in jesting with her with
  5. jests that continuing to do them leads to no good.
  6. He continues (to act) in this way even
  7. now in spite that the aforementioned woman detests this matter. A petition about this
  8. [was written] to the writer of these lines and was thrown to his [dwelling]
  9. without him knowing who wrote it. The situation required informing …
  10. our lord … the prince of all the diaspora …
  11. of this scandal and apprising him of the petition. Following this, the aforementioned prince sat
  12. ...
  13. in Cairo in the house of his sister....
