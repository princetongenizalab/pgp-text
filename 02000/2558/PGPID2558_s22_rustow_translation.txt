His servant Yosef b. Maḥfūẓ.
In (your) na(me), O Mer(ciful One).
I bless God, to whom greatness belongs, and give thanks to Him with praise and with song, He who is mighty and exalted, great and abundant in His goodness, He who
cannot be searched out, who sees all and is seen by every living thing, who preceded all creation,
whose majesty and might are in the heavens. They are great and beyond reckoning. Our lord and the crown of our heads
will comfort you, my heart, the light of my heart and the light of my eyes, Samuel ha-Nagid, nagid of the treasured people. May God be his help and shield him in His shelter,
magnify his honor, save him from the violence of the government, bless every work of his hands, bless, guard and help him,
and exalt the splendor of all his dear ones. Amen. Your excellency’s servant, grateful for your bounty and [proclaimer] of some of your virtues,
informs you of some of what befell me at the hands of Abū Saʿīd Ibn Fustuqa,
about whom your excellency asked me. I approached him and drew near to his high status for three months
until I obtained for him a rescript (tawqīʿ) cancelling his capitation tax (jizya). Then, afterwards, he turned
the jurist (faqīh) against us, and also our lord the chief judge (qāḍī al-quḍāt), and all the Muslims.
He paid a dinar to someone in my presence, but (that person) could not fulfill his request. Then he brought
me into the Islamic legal system (al-sharīʿa) and sued me for the whole amount, and he made me lose the fee of a court messenger, two dirhams.
Because of how hard he pressed me and complained that I had afflicted them (?) [. . .]
Then they were the cause of what happened to me with the Ḥaver Yeshuʿa. He informed against me
to the tax collectors. He went after me because I had a musallaqa (? or muṭallaqa, divorcée?), and she had converted to Islam. I came to check on
my son at her house on Saturday, and they meant to burn me (?). Yet I repaid him with kindness and what was incumbent upon me.
