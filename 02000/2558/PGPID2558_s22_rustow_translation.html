Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: Bodl. MS heb. d 66/97

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. His servant Yosef b. Maḥfūẓ.
  2. In (your) na(me), O Mer(ciful One).
  3. I bless God, to whom greatness belongs, and give thanks to Him with praise and with song, He who is mighty and exalted, great and abundant in His goodness, He who
  4. cannot be searched out, who sees all and is seen by every living thing, who preceded all creation,
  5. whose majesty and might are in the heavens. They are great and beyond reckoning. Our lord and the crown of our heads
  6. will comfort you, my heart, the light of my heart and the light of my eyes, Samuel ha-Nagid, nagid of the treasured people. May God be his help and shield him in His shelter,
  7. magnify his honor, save him from the violence of the government, bless every work of his hands, bless, guard and help him,
  8. and exalt the splendor of all his dear ones. Amen. Your excellency’s servant, grateful for your bounty and [proclaimer] of some of your virtues,
  9. informs you of some of what befell me at the hands of Abū Saʿīd Ibn Fustuqa,
  10. about whom your excellency asked me. I approached him and drew near to his high status for three months
  11. until I obtained for him a rescript (tawqīʿ) cancelling his capitation tax (jizya). Then, afterwards, he turned
  12. the jurist (faqīh) against us, and also our lord the chief judge (qāḍī al-quḍāt), and all the Muslims.
  13. He paid a dinar to someone in my presence, but (that person) could not fulfill his request. Then he brought
  14. me into the Islamic legal system (al-sharīʿa) and sued me for the whole amount, and he made me lose the fee of a court messenger, two dirhams.
  15. Because of how hard he pressed me and complained that I had afflicted them (?) [. . .]
  16. Then they were the cause of what happened to me with the Ḥaver Yeshuʿa. He informed against me
  17. to the tax collectors. He went after me because I had a musallaqa (? or muṭallaqa, divorcée?), and she had converted to Islam. I came to check on
  18. my son at her house on Saturday, and they meant to burn me (?). Yet I repaid him with kindness and what was incumbent upon me.

Recto, right margin

  1. After that, when the jāliya collectors
  2. learned (of it), they said to me: “Don’t issue
  3. a rescript, and don’t trouble yourself. This man has done
  4. evil, and if you do anything for him, we will annul it
  5. by stating that he has means and a house.”
  6. I was taken aback. Then he wrote
  7. a petition (ruqʿa) about it to the jurist (faqīh) containing
  8. (a charge of?) murder. Then I owed them
  9. a deposit. They claimed against me
  10. that it was a pledge (rahn), and they made me swear
  11. before the jurist that it was
  12. a deposit. They claimed that they had
  13. twenty dirhams owed to them by me.
  14. The oath was settled, and
  15. a messenger came out with me and made me forfeit
  16. six dirhams as the jurist's fee (?)
  17. charged to me, and the messenger’s charged to me. Peace be upon you.
