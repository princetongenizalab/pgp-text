Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S 10J10.10

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto, top

  1. “For surely there is a future, and your hope shall not be cut off” (Prov 23:18).
  2. An honored elder, endowed with blessings, adorned with a crown of glory, as one who wears linen breeches (Lev 6:3), who is unique;
  3. the precious dignitary, chosen and tested, who shines in righteousness like the light of morning; who walks in his integrity (Prov 20:7) and gives of his bread to the poor (Prov 22:9);
  4. who purifies his body, makes himself worthy, is fitting in his station, eloquent in his speech, and beloved among his people. May his God be with him (Ezra 1:3), to guard him and strengthen him
  5. by night and by day, when he lies down and when he rises (Deut 6:7), to be with him in trouble. May He who dwells on high and (whose) footstool (is the earth) (cf. Isa 66:1) make his peace abound like a river (Isa 48:18),
  6. guard him and sustain him, and repay him with good recompense; lead him in paths of righteousness for His name’s sake (Ps 23:3); hide him on the day of His wrath; and may he be worthy
  7. to behold His pleasantness (Ps 27:4) and the completion of the building of His Temple. As the One who loves His people (said): “I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him and honor him. With length of days I will satisfy him,
  8. and show him My salvation” (Ps 91:15–16). To the honor of his glorious and precious eminence, our master Netanʾel the Elder, a restorer for his generation, head of the congregation, father
  9. of the destitute and provider for the poor, who opens his hand (Deut 15:8) with a cheerful countenance (Avot 1:15). May he find life, righteousness and honor (Prov 21:21) in this world
  10. and in the world to come. May he be worthy to see his precious ones, who meditate on sound wisdom, rise in their paths, fulfill their joy, and grant their hope
  11. in his lifetime. His h(onor), g(reatness and) h(oliness), our master and teacher Yakhīn, glory of the congregation, whose name and tent shall be blessed with the blessing of every prophet,
  12. and all who see them shall recognize that they are a seed the Lord has blessed (Isa 61:9). So may it be (His) will.

Recto, bottom (Arabic script)

  1. If I were to describe the longing I feel for the presence of my lord, the most illustrious elder, may God perpetuate his exaltation and elevation, it would be long and beyond description. I never cease (offering) fond mention (of him),
  2. praise, and righteous prayer at the times of prayer. I ask Him, exalted be His attributes, to hear from me righteous prayers for him and his children, and not to disappoint(?) …(?) among them,
  3. by His mercy. The bearer of these lines to his presence, may God exalt it, my lord Abū Saʿd, is Yakhīn the poet, may his Rock preserve him. He has settled in al-Maḥalla
  4. and he is a Khaybarī. The lord of al-Maḥalla harassed him and demanded the kharāj from him, so he fled from him and left his family living(?) there. He has with him documents from … (?)
  5. He has asked me to ask your excellency to be generous to him from the overflow of your bounty, by obtaining(?) for him a document validating his (other) documents, and to manage (the matter)
  6. with your sound and successful judgment, and to treat him as he deserves; he is worthy of kindness. Your excellency’s judgment is supreme in whatever you see fit.

Recto, right margin

  1. Your friend kisses your hand.
  2. The group sends greetings to your excellency
  3. and kisses your hands.
  4. Abu l-Faḍl and …… 
  5. (Your presence) is singled out by its friend
  6. with the finest greetings.
  7. God alone suffices for me.

Verso, address, right column

  1. (To) the presence of my lo[rd] the most illustrious elder Abū Saʿd Hibatallāh b. Yefet,
  2. head of the congregation[s?], may God perpetuate his exaltation and elevation.

Verso, address, left column

  1. (From) the one who is grateful for his favor, Ibrāhīm b. Isḥāq
  2. al-Ḥasanī(?), may (I) be made his ransom. (To) Cairo, may she be guarded, God willing.
