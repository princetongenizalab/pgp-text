“For surely there is a future, and your hope shall not be cut off” (Prov 23:18).
An honored elder, endowed with blessings, adorned with a crown of glory, as one who wears linen breeches (Lev 6:3), who is unique;
the precious dignitary, chosen and tested, who shines in righteousness like the light of morning; who walks in his integrity (Prov 20:7) and gives of his bread to the poor (Prov 22:9);
who purifies his body, makes himself worthy, is fitting in his station, eloquent in his speech, and beloved among his people. May his God be with him (Ezra 1:3), to guard him and strengthen him
by night and by day, when he lies down and when he rises (Deut 6:7), to be with him in trouble. May He who dwells on high and (whose) footstool (is the earth) (cf. Isa 66:1) make his peace abound like a river (Isa 48:18),
guard him and sustain him, and repay him with good recompense; lead him in paths of righteousness for His name’s sake (Ps 23:3); hide him on the day of His wrath; and may he be worthy
to behold His pleasantness (Ps 27:4) and the completion of the building of His Temple. As the One who loves His people (said): “I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him and honor him. With length of days I will satisfy him,
and show him My salvation” (Ps 91:15–16). To the honor of his glorious and precious eminence, our master Netanʾel the Elder, a restorer for his generation, head of the congregation, father
of the destitute and provider for the poor, who opens his hand (Deut 15:8) with a cheerful countenance (Avot 1:15). May he find life, righteousness and honor (Prov 21:21) in this world
and in the world to come. May he be worthy to see his precious ones, who meditate on sound wisdom, rise in their paths, fulfill their joy, and grant their hope
in his lifetime. His h(onor), g(reatness and) h(oliness), our master and teacher Yakhīn, glory of the congregation, whose name and tent shall be blessed with the blessing of every prophet,
and all who see them shall recognize that they are a seed the Lord has blessed (Isa 61:9). So may it be (His) will.
