Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S NS J287

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. [ … and you shall] lie down, and none shall make you afraid, and many shall entreat your favor (Job 11:19).
  2. For the Lord shall be your confidence and shall keep your foot from being caught (Prov. 3:26).
  3. The Lord shall keep you from all evil; He shall keep your soul.
  4. The Lord shall keep your going out and your coming in from this time forth and
  5. forevermore (Ps. 121:7–8). For length of days and years of life and peace
  6. shall they add to you (Prov. 3:2). And you shall find favor and good understanding in the sight of God
  7. and man (Prov. 3:4).
  8. The slave of his excellency our gaʾon, may his majesty be exalted and his honor increased, […], reports to
  9. his loftiness, may God perpetuate his majesty, what the excellency already knows
  10. regarding the affair of Abū l-Ḥasan al-Ṭaḥḥān (the miller) in al-[Q]āhira al-Muʿizziyya (Cairo). She asks
  11. and implores before him that, if a contract (kitāb) is concluded between them, it should contain these
  12. conditions as a safeguard against its consequences and against the mischief (ghāʾila) of his family t[oward her]:
  13. that she shall have neʾemanut (trusted status) fully and completely, binding on him and on [his] h[eirs]
  14. after him. .. She is to reside in Miṣr [Fusṭāṭ] until Passover. He has said that he wants
  15. [ … ] and he has not specified anything for me, nor has he paid anything of what is need[ed]
  16. [fo]r it by way of upkeep [ … ]
  17. [ … ] her hands and [ … ]
  18. its cost, not I [ … ]
  19. the generous [ … ]
  20. [ … ]

Recto, right margin

  1. … that her deferred marriage payment remain in [her] hand;
  2. that no work be demanded of her;
  3. and if she does work, her earnings belong to her;
  4. and he shall not have the right to say to her,
  5. "Embroider in people's houses and bring me
  6. the proceeds," or anything similar.
  7. And to his excellency,
  8. may G[o]d perpetuate his majesty, belongs the exalted judgment
  9. in this matter.
  10. May your peace
  11. increase forever.

Recto, top margin

  1. The slave (i.e., the humble petitioner), wife of
  2. Ibn al-Maghāzilī
