Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S NS J277

S. D. Goitein, unpublished editions.

  1. From his servant Abu 'l-ʿAlāʾ
  2. b. Abu 'l-Faḍl
  3. al-ʿAnīnī
  4. Happy are they that are upright, who walk in the law of the Lord. Happy is the man
  5. who fears the Lord, who delights greatly in His commandments. To His Excellency our Lord, the light
  6. of our eyes, the delight of our hearts and the crown of our head, the Prince of Princes
  7. and Nagid of all the Negidim, may his name last forever and may God preserve his two
  8. sons, the noble princes, and fulfil through them the scripture: "Instead of
  9. your fathers shall be your sons, you shall make them princes throughout
  10. the land." This is to inform His Excellency, our lord — may God establish
  11. his glory forever — that I am a man unable to press his case, whose life is
  12. in danger. I work in a silkweaver's workshop
  13. together with a number of Muslim laborers, one of whom is a Jewish apostate.
  14. This man persecutes me and seeks to destroy me, for he wants to oust me
  15. from the workshop. Time and again he has said to me, "you swear falsely by the King
  16. all day long," and he has already made a false deposition to this effect.
  17. Your servant is unable to quit the workshop, for I owe a debt
  18. to my employer. Therefore I ask God, the Exalted, and your Excellency,
  19. to save my soul from death, by issuing an order to
  20. the judges to allow me to pay the debt in installments, before I am
  21. imprisoned. May your Excellency make this effort in order to
  22. save me, as is your custom with any son of Israel. May your wellbeing
  23. be increased.
