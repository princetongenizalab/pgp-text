The esteemed community living  in Fustat 
-4. may their Rock bless them, a(men) n(esah) s(elah), are  hereby informed that the community has received a letter from our lord  the Nagid, l(ong) m(ay he live), 

greeting you and praying for you and your children, 
men, women, children and young men, and ordering  you with utmost
command to be of one heart, to love one 
-9.  another, to strive hard to perform the religious commandments, which  as is well known from your charitableness you are accustomed to doing.  

No one shall transgress the authority of the religious law or 
go  to the gentiles' (courts). Whoever transgresses and goes 
to the gentile courts prior to appearing before the Jewish court for "informers"  
in Cairo and receiving their permission is under a 
curse and subject to a warning, and again, a warning against disobedience. 
May God the exal(ted) be your succor and make your habitations  prosper, 
and may you not exclude us from your thoughts. And peace. 
[postscript]: Read them in the l(arge) synagogue of the  J(erusalemites) on Thursday, 
Friday, and on the Sabbath, and send them to us on 
Sunday. And peace.
