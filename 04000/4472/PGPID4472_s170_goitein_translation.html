English Translation of Letter: CUL Or.1080 J42

S. D. Goitein, Letters of Medieval Jewish Traders (Princeton University Press, 1973).

Recto

  1. I am writing to you, my lord, the elder—may God prolong your life and make your honored position and prosperity permanent—
  2. from Tyre on Dhū l-Qaʿda 16. I am well, praise to God
  3. alone. I hope my letters have arrived and that you have read
  4. them. I informed you about what I had sent with Salāma b. Jaysh and what I
  5. paid for the transport, namely 5 1/2 dinars per camel load, plus payments to the guard,
  6. 1/8 dinar per camel load. You are not unfamiliar
  7. with the security situation in Damascus and the whole of Syria. When, by God's favor—may he be glorified—
  8. the camel driver arrived in Tyre, he was induced by Abū l-Aʿlā Ibn Abī Ḥaffāẓ
  9. to unload the paper, for the latter wished to get hold of his partnerships,
  10. take the paper, and forward it on his own. A man from
  11. Aleppo, called Abū Muḥammad the Aleppan, traveled in the caravan with goods of the size of your partnership consignment—
  12. I do not know the exact number of the camel loads,
  13. but I have no doubt that he paid the same freight as I did, namely 5 1/2 dinars
  14. per load. He, too, left them in Tyre. My lords, the elders in Damascus, received a letter from my master
  15. Abū l-Aʿlā, the agent, informing them that he
  16. had received from Salāma b. Jaysh 15 dinars. He had carried twenty-eight
  17. camel loads for us, seven for the partnership with Abū Naṣr,
  18. and twenty-one loads for our partnership. I wrote you, my lord, may God preserve
  19. your honored position, to get hold of Salāma b. Jaysh and sue him
  20. for the balance. My lord, the elder Abū l-Ḥusayn also wrote
  21. a number of letters to my lord and master Abū Naṣr. When, however, I arrived in Tyre,
  22. two letters from my master Abū Naṣr Ṣadaqa arrived there which put me
  23. and him to shame. He writes
  24. that I had informed him that I had paid a freight of
  25. 4 1/2 dinars per load to Salāma b. Jaysh. In another letter he says:
  26. "I know that the camel driver paid the entire freight due from him in Tyre."
  27. And this after I had written to him every week to get together with
  28. you, my lord, and to cooperate in suing the camel driver.
  29. Nothing has come out of this. He should have asked the camel drivers, he should have asked you,

Verso

  1. he does not have money." This is the talk of an irresponsible person. If possible, please
  2. meet him and explain to him what is proper. Also, please sue the camel driver for the balance
  3. and be circumspect and exert yourself to sell what I have sent either for our partnership or on my personal account,
  4. for, after my arrival in Fustat, I shall stay only a short time, if God wills. You will put me greatly in your debt
  5. by this and earn my thanks and good prayers.
  6. And act immediately. A little over twenty bales of paper from a partnership of mine had remained in Tyre.
  7. I sent them to Ramla, and they have arrived there safely. I also have sent
  8. fifteen bales from a partnership with you and shall, God willing, carry all the merchandise with me.
  9. Kindly inform me whether you have received
  10. a letter from my lord, the elder Abū l-Ḥusayn, stating that the rose marmalade
  11. and the lubricating oil are on my private account. Also, let me know what God has granted you to sell.
  12. Write me a letter every week, and in Hebrew script, please.
  13. If you have any order to make, please honor me with it. And may your well-being wax.
  14. (P.S.) If it is difficult to sell the paper or anything else by weight, and you prefer to sell it at retail,
  15. do whatever your propitious judgment suggests to you. But, God willing, act without procrastination.
  16. Kindly inform me, my lord, about the price of Nizāriyya money coined in Damascus,
  17. for a friend gave me some to sell on my journey. Also the price of the rose marmalade.
  18. My master Abū l-Barakāt al-Sharābī, who has lapis lazuli here in Tyre,
  19. has five loads of (dried) plums, which find no buyer, in Acre. If plums have a good price (in Fustat),
  20. write me if he prefers to transport by land, in which case it will cost first 2 (dinars)
  21. per load from Acre to Ramla by sea and the total price to Fustat will come to 5
  22. dinars, more or less. Kindly inform me what I shall do in this matter. God alone suffices me.

Verso - address

  1. (Address in Arabic characters: same as in no. 15)
  2. (Note of postal agency:) (From) Najā al-Anṣārī (To) ʿAlī b. Muḥammad and his two sons Muḥammad and Ḥusayn
  3. Please answer immediately.
