English Translation of State document: ENA 3941.4

Jane Hathaway, Ottoman-Era Documents from the Cairo Genizah (Open Book Publishers, 2026).

Recto

  1. Deposits

  2. from the hand of the exemplar of the great and support of

    the notables, of power and renown, his excellency

  3. our master the noble amīr al-Ḥājj ʿUmar Agha, supervisor of the silk looms for the port of Alexandria. That [amount]

  4. was collected by Ibrāhīm, the gatekeeper of Wakālat al- Bīṭāsh, as his salary, registered

  5. in the Bureau of Silk Looms in the aforementioned port for the month of Jumād[ā al-]Ākhir, the year 1239, thirty-nine [~ February 1824].

Below

  1. Month: 1
  2. twenty

  3. pāras      ghurūsh [?]

  4.                      six
  5. 20                  6

Below

  1. Six Rūmī ghurūsh and twenty pāras only, nothing else.

Witness

  1. Aḥmad Ghānim al-Zabīdī [?] the ḥücreci [?] (chamber-supervisor)

  2. bore witness to that.
