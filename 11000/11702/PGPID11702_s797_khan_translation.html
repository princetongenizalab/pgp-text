English Translation of State document: ENA 3974.4

Geoffrey Khan, "A Petition to the Fāṭimid Caliph al-Āmir from the Cairo Genizah Concerning an Inheritance," in Orientalistische Studien zu Sprache und Literatur Festgabe zum 65. Geburtstag von Werner Diem (Harrassowitz, 2011), 175-186.

Recto

  1. The servant and slave 

  2. Manṣūr ibn Salāma from among the people of Sindiyūn.

  3. In the name of God, the Merciful and Compassionate. 

  4. The blessings and benedictions of God, and his increasing benefactions and most excellent greetings upon our lord and master the imām al-Āmir 

  5. bi-Aḥkām Allāh, Commander of the Faithful, and upon his pure ancestors and his noble sons. The slave kisses the ground

  6. before the most noble presence, may God double its lights and exalt its beacon, and reports that mercy and compassion embraces

  7. everybody and that the father of the slave has been silent in the countryside in Maḥallat Salīm ibn Sahl and has gone away from his hometown. The slave has 

  8. in the district known as Sandiyūn of the province of al-Muzāḥamiyya a house enclosure, which used to belong to his mother. She has died and the slave has succeeded her ⟦and he asks⟧ 

  9. and he has continued to dwell in it, and nobody has been found who has a greater right to it than he. So, he makes a request in humility from the all-embracing mercies and generous kindnesses for a noble rescript (to be written) on the back of his petition to the prosperous judge 

  10. in charge of jurisdiction in al-Maḥalla and the surrounding region, with a copy to be deposited in his (i.e., the petitioner’s) hand, (instructing him) to register (the house enclosure) as the property of the slave, in the way of property that has been certified as valid with him, thus vindicating his right so that he may live in these splendid days,

  11. thus showibg him benificence and kindness. To our lord, God’s blessings be upon him, belongs the exalted opinion in this matter, if God wills.

  12. Praise be to God alone, and his blessings and peace upon our Lord Muḥammad our prophet and his family
