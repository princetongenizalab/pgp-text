Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of State document: ENA 3974.4

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

 

  1. The servant and slave

  2. Manṣūr ibn Ṣadaqa from the people of Sindiyyūn.

  3. In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate.

  4. The blessings and benedictions of God and his increasing benefactions and most excellent greetings upon our lord and master the imām al-Āmir 

  5. bi-Aḥkām Allāh, Commander of the Faithful, and upon his pure ancestors and his noble sons. The slave kisses the ground

  6. before the most noble presence, may God double his lights and exalt his beacon, and reports that mercy and compassion have embraced

  7. everyone, and that the father of the slave has been a ḍāmin in the countryside in Maḥallat Salīm ibn Sahl and has left his hometown, and that the slave

  8. has a house enclosure in the district known as Sindiyyūn, in the province of al-Muzāḥamiyya, that \\used to// belong to his mother. She has died, and the slave is her heir. He asks 

  9. \\He has lived there continuously, and no one has more right to it than he. He asks// and beseeches (him of) the all-embracing mercies and generous kindnesses for a noble rescript (to be written) on the back of his petition to the qāḍī al-muwaffaq 

  10. with jurisdiction over al-Maḥalla and the surrounding region, \\with a copy to be deposited in his (the petitioner’s) hand//, instructing him to register the slave’s property \\in the manner in which property is registered with him as valid,// thus conveying his right to him, so that he may live in these splendid days,

  11. as a benefaction and a favor to him. To our lord, God’s blessings be upon him, belongs the exalted opinion in this matter, if God wills.

  12. Praise be to God alone, and his blessings and peace upon our lord Muḥammad, our prophet, and his family.
