English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J17.30

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. [. . .] let your “house” (i.e., wife) and the “house” (wife) of the beadle Sulaymān collect
  2. –3. from the women. In addition, you alone should make the collection. Exert yourself in this. Whatever is
  4. obt[ained] from everyone, keep with you until we inform you
  5. what to do. We know about this matter
  6. only from you. Do not make it necessary for us to reprove you.
  7. Beware of being disobedient. May God the ex(alted) be
  8. your succor, and may you be inscribed in the book of [lif]e, the book
  9. of remembrance. And peace.
