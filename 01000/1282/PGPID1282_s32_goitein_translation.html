English Translation of Letter: T-S Ar.7.13

S. D. Goitein, "Side Lights on Jewish Education from the Cairo Geniza," in Gratz College Anniversary volume (Gratz College, 1971), 83-110.

Recto

  1. Your son Elijah b. Zechariah
  2. To the most illustrious elder, the illustrious Kohen Abu ʾl-ʿAlāʾ, m(ay his) R(ock) k(eep him).
  3. If you wish to revive the knowledge of the
  4. sections of the Bible which you have studied — your servant is at your disposal. And also at
  5. that of your father, the Dean of the Kohens, (may his) R(ock) k(eep him). If coming
  6. to me causes you embarrassment and shame, I am prepared to come to your noble home....
