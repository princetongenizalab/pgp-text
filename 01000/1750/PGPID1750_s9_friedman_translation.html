English Translation of Legal document: T-S 18J1.3

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

recto

  1. On the first day of the week, which is the eleventh day of the month Tamuz, year four
  2. thousand, seven hundred and sixty-seven A.M., which is year one thousand, three
  3. hundred and eighteen, of the era by which we are accustomed to count in Fustat, Egypt,
  4. which is situated on the Nile river, Israel b. Daniel betrothed Sittūna, the virgin,
  5. who is of age, daughter of David, represented by her father David b. Abraham, after his
  6. agency had been verified by two witnesses—namely David b. R. Shemaʿya the elder and Khalaf b.
  7. Abraham—with a qinyan [to affirm] that she had consented to his agency to give her in marriage to this Israel. And the witnesses
  8. were acquainted with her. The betrothal is for qiddush with a marriage gift of 250
  9. good, fully weighed dinars. At the time of her betrothal [qiddush], he gave her 100 dinars, which is
  10. the total advanced payment. There remain [as a debt] incumbent upon him 150 good, fully weighed dinars,
  11. which is the total delayed payment. And this Israel b. Daniel undertook to nourish her,
  12. to maintain and esteem [her], when she enters his home. And she undertook, this Sittūna daughter of
  13. David, to attend him in purity and cleanness, after she enters the marriage chamber.
  14. With this understanding, the two sides agreed, Israel, the betrothed, b. Daniel
  15. and David, the agent, b. Abraham. And this Israel gave to David, his father-in-law,
  16. the agent, in our presence, 100 dinars, the advanced mohar, and the rings with which he effected
  17. the qiddushin. This David, the agent, b. Abraham, received them
  18. in our presence. And the two of them instructed the scribe to write and the witnesses to testify.
  19. We performed a complete and strict qinyan with them, with the consent of both of them, with a proper implement
  20. for performing it. Strong and valid. Jepthah ha-Kohen b. Toviah.
  21. Samuel b. Ḥanokh [whose] s[oul is at] r[est]. Neḥuma b. Wahb.
  22. Muʿammar, the scribe, b. Isaac [whose] s[oul is at] r[est].

verso

A marriage contract for betrothal
