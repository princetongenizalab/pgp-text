English Translation of Legal document: T-S 12.154

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. By the name of the Lord. May we prosper in what we do. They built and prospered. The elders of Judea build and prosper.
  2. On the fifth day (of the week), in the month of Av, sixteen days therein, year 4705
  3. A.M., in the town Qugandim(a), which is on the river of Egypt, this marriage deed was written. “I, Hillel b. Joseph
  4. (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) declare, intentionally and of my own choice, to take this Mubāraka, the virgin, the bride, daughter of Abraham,
  5. (may his) e(nd be) g(ood), to be my wife, to take her in qiddushin, and (for her) to attend me in purity and modesty, in cleanness and sanctity,
  6. As the modest and respectable daughters of Israel (do) with their husbands, in sanctity.” He undertook, this Hillel
  7. b. Joseph (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) to nourish, provide for, clothe and cover, esteem, honor this Mubāraka, the virgin,
  8. The bride, daughter of Abraham, (may his) e(nd be) g(ood), as the sons of Israel who conduct themselves with their wives in the manner of Jewish men. This
  9. Hillel b. Joseph, the groom, betrothed (qiddesh) this Mubāraka daughter of Abraham, the virgin, the bride, with six gold pieces.
  10. Of them he gave her two full gold pieces at the time of the betrothal (qiddush), and there remain “on his neck” four
  11. \\”Israelite”// gold pieces. Isaac b. Yeshuʿa (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) her agent, received payment. And she consented to all that he do[es] in her affair. This
  12. Isaac b. Yeshuʿa took upon himself the agency with (in the presence of) two trustworthy witnesses, by her [in]structions,
  13. from beginning to end. They built and prospered.
  14. This is what she brought in with her: a white mantle and a white gown–4 dinars, a sleeping mat and a quilt–2 dinars,
  15. A red scarf and two headdresses–1 dinar, two pillows–1 dinar, a table and all that goes with it–eight
  16. and ⅓ dinar. The total of what she brought in with her is 16 ⅓ dinars.
  17. Ḥur ha-Kohen b. Abraham, (may his) e(nd be) g(ood), witness. I, Samuel b. Joseph, witness.
  18. Joseph b. Ṣedaqa ha-Levi, witness. I, Solomon b. Faraj, witness.
  19. I, Jacob b. Binyām, witness. ⟦Hiba b. I, Sam⟧ Samue[l] b. Hiba.
  20. Amram b. Abraham, witness. Khalaf b. Bishr, witness.
  21. Mevorakh b. Najm, witness. Shemarya b. Naʿmān, witness.
  22. Hillel b. Joseph, the groom, witness. I, Isaac b. Faraj, witness.
