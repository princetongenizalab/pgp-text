English Translation of Legal document: T-S 12.119

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. [... dinar]s; they were included in her dowry. [And] there remain incumbent upon him fifty d[inars ...]
  2. [... She has no r]ight to claim of them one per[ū]ṭa, as long as he is alive and fulfills [her] three [needs ...]  
  3. [...] five [...] a pair of ringlets—four <dinars>, a silver ...—one dinar, a pair of earrings and a gold pin—two [dinars ...]
  4. [... a s]kullcap [...]-one dinar, a Tall skullcap-one dinar, two garments with borders of another color and a coat made of different materials—two dinars [...]  
  5. [...—one] dinar [... a] dark blue [s]kullcap-two dinars, a perfumed skullcap and a red k'twnyh—one dinar, a shawl [...]  
  6. [...]s—two dinars, a bed cover and four cushions-three dinars, a mat—one dinar, two pairs of ʿAbbadā[nī] mats—[...]  
  7. [...] forty and one half dinars. She also has one third of a house, mixed, not divided, of the new house kn[own as ...]  
  8. [... to the south ... al-gha]ḍāʾirī to the east, the city wall; to the north, the street, into which the door opens; and to the west, a house of abomination ...]  
  9. [... Oba]diah b. Halfon (who) r(ests in) E(den) with consent from him and from her, without compulsion and without coercion, rather by his choice and his desire. And [we] performed a qi[nyan …]
  10. [...] her mother to her, an apartment, in which she will dwell as long as her mother is alive. And after she will d[ie...] 
  11. [... she will ...] and acquire, dwell, and allow others to dwell, rent out, mortgage, sell, dedicate and declare holy, and do all that she [desires ...]  
  12. [...] And Turfa, the bride's mother, performed a [qi]nyan of herself, with an implement suitable for this (affirming) that she gave the third of the house ...  
  13. [...] in the aforementioned third <of the house> and whatever the house requires, of repairs or damages, Obadiah and Solomon, [the] groom, will [...]  
  14. [...] his heirs after him, to make payment to her from the best, choice properties, possession, and money, which he has under the whole sky  [...] 
  15. [... nor like for]mularies, rather according to the stringencies of all ketubba deeds enacted by the sages (may their) m(emory) b(e a blessing) and in common practice. And their practice is according to the prac[tice ...]  
  16. [... and all the stipulations of the To]rah and the court stipulations, from beginning to end. And we performed with them a complete, strict qinyan, with an implement suitable [for this ...]  
  17. [... whether in] the statutes of our lords, the sages or in the laws of the Gentiles. May they build and prosper. May they sprout [like] a palm tree and [shine] like the su[n ...]  
  18. Nathan b. Elisha (whose) s(oul is at) r(est). Ṣedaqa b. Isaac (who) r(ests in) E(den).
