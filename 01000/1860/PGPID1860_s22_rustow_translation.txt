Recto
To be delivered to Ibrāhīm al-Nakhkhās (the slave dealer):
I am writing so that he may buy for the Nezer
the slave girl (waṣīfa) and take her (and) d[eposit her]
with whoever is convenient until you come downriver (to Fusṭāṭ?),
(then) bring her with you. Whatever you spend
on her for provisions, for hire of
a boat, or anything else will be reimbursed
to you. If anything is left over from her price,
pay it from your own (funds), and take [her (?)]
with you, and deposit [         ]
Yūsuf, his associate [           ]
