Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S 8J10.9

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. To be delivered to Ibrāhīm al-Nakhkhās (the slave dealer):
  2. I am writing so that he may buy for the Nezer
  3. the slave girl (waṣīfa) and take her (and) d[eposit her]
  4. with whoever is convenient until you come downriver (to Fusṭāṭ?),
  5. (then) bring her with you. Whatever you spend
  6. on her for provisions, for hire of
  7. a boat, or anything else will be reimbursed
  8. to you. If anything is left over from her price,
  9. pay it from your own (funds), and take [her (?)]
  10. with you, and deposit [         ]
  11. Yūsuf, his associate [           ]
