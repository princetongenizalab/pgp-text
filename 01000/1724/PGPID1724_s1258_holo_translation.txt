Recto
In the Name of the Merciful One
God … peace unto his people, may He spread the canopy of His peace, in which all those who seek refuge in Him will be protected. On it
will be…all the sacrifices, and in it will be fulfilled all the blessings on our brethren, our beloved ones, the ones dear to us, and [colleagues]
of our hearts, [we long to see you] … the holy congregation of those living in the land of Greece, (they are the holy congregation, the community of M[ara]thea) who are dispersed in the field of the Edomites, subject
to the power of the enemy, handed over to the yoke of the persecutors, bearing the [brunt] of the taskmasters’ staffs. May God help them and bless them.
May He have mercy on them and shine His countenance upon them, selah. May He hoist a banner before the nations, to gather them in to His [i.e., their] inheritance and to His place of repose, there
where He yearned for it [i.e., His seat] to be, with all that is written [in Scripture] for a life of joy and happiness for the redeemed ones of the Lord, as He reassured [us in Scripture:]
