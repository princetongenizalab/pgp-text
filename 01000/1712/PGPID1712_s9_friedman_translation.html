English Translation of Legal document: T-S 16.169

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. [...] situated on the shore of the sea
  2. [.... of his] own [choice …] to be married to this Ḥusn, the virgin, 
  3. [... f]eed and s[upport … in the manner of Jewish men, who fee]d and support and … their wives faithfully.
  4. [... And Ḥusn,] the bride, daughter of [Mardūk harkened to] Khalaf, [the groom], b. Maḥfūẓ (whose) s(oul is at) r(est) (and agreed) to be his wife and to
  5. [serve him … in the m]anner of wives, the daughters of Isra[el, who att]end, show honor, and esteem their husbands in purity and sanctity. And he took
  6. [... Ḥ]usn, the virgin, the bride, daughter of Mardūk (whose) s(oul is at) r(est), in complete qiddushin with two hundred zuz, as the holy college had established.
  7. [...] The two of them consented, in the presence of trustworthy Jewish men, valid witnesses, that her mohar be thirty-five dinars of pure, good quality gold.  
  8. [...] And he has already presented to her, at the time of her betrothal, five dinars. And there remain incumbent upon him thirty dinars of good quality gold, with the insignia of the temporal ruler. 
  9. [...He gave] her from his money an Egyptian sheet, an absolute and permanent gift. And this is what she brought from the Merciful and from the house of her fat[hers:]  
  10. [...] dyeing vessel, a water container and a mortar—ten dina[rs ...]
