English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J17.27

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

Recto

  1. "While you shall be (called) Priests of the Lord," etc. (Isaiah 61:6)
  2. [...Your slave...] kisses the ground before (our) m(aster) and t(eacher) Joseph ha-Kohen the beloved Dignitary,
  3. Crown of the Priests, Diadem of the Dignitaries, and informs you that I am [going through] adversity that none
  4. but the Creator of all existence knows. I am hiding out in the house
  5. like the women. I cannot go out except [in the] evening. I am fleeing
  6. from a debt that I owe. I am unable to do any gainful work
  7. unless I go out. My little ones are dying of hunger because
  8. I have been hiding out. Our lord the Head promised me the pledges of a week (jumʿa).
  9. When the week I was to have claimed the pledge that he
  10. promised arrived, I was told that it had gone to the elder Bū Saʿd the cantor, (may his) Rock) p(rotect him).
  11. I therefore ask my master please to intercede on my behalf so that I am paid
  12. that w(eek's pledges), for I have heard that nothing is accomplished without the decision of
  13. my master, may God grant you success. I am all the more dependent upon you.
  14. And peace.
