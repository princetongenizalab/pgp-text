English Translation of Legal document: T-S 12.480

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. this bri[de ...]
  2. b. Moses [her] agen[t, by the testimony of two witnesse]s, [who are]
  3. Mevorakh ha-Kohen b. Joseph [and Eli ha-Levi b.] Aaron. [And with this understanding]  
  4. the two sides [consented; and they instructed the scribe to [write and the signatories]

  6. for this. And we wrote and signed so that it be between them as a title of rights [and as proof]  
  7. Eli ha-Levi b. Aaron (whose) s(oul is at) r(est). Azhar ha-Levi Shabbat. Mevorakh ha-Kohen [b. Joseph]
  8. Solomon ha Ko[hen ...]  
  9. ha-Levi \b./ Isaac.     Mansūr ha-Levi b. Solomon, w(itness).

  11. Moses b. Amram, w(itness).
