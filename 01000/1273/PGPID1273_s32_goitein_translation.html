English Translation of Letter: T-S Ar.53.65

S. D. Goitein, "Side Lights on Jewish Education from the Cairo Geniza," in Gratz College Anniversary volume (Gratz College, 1971), 83-110.

Recto

  1. This is to inform you my lord, the elder,
  2. may God make your honored position permanent,
  3. that Abu ʾl-Ḥasan, after his
  4. transfer to my school,
  5. showed great zeal for study.
  6. His great misfortune was that Abu ʾl-Ḥasan b.
  7. Wuhayb broke his board
  8. with the knowledge of the other boys. And peace.
