English Translation of Legal document: T-S 12.630

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

verso

  1. [This is the] marriage contract that Fuhayd b. [...] executed
  2. [for Badriyya] daughter of ʿAllūn (whose) s(oul is at) r(est)
  3. [...] Save! According to the law of Moses  
  4. [...] May they prosper
