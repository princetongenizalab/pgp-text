English Translation of Legal document: T-S AS 146.66

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

A

  1. [...s]ix days in the month of Nisan, year 3 of the sabbatical cycle, which is year 4 thousand, six hundred and ninety-three
  2. [A.M] in Damascus, Samuel b. Abraham the Persian gave a ring of security for Khayriya, the virgin, daughter of Ezra 
  3. [... according to] the custom of the small synagogue, of the Babylonians. They fixed between themselves (a mohar of) fifty gold pieces: twenty-five, 
  4. [the cus]tom of the con[gregation] and the remainder an addition for mohar. At the time of this security he advanced three fully weighed good gold pieces;
  5. [and] when he marries her he shall pay to her seventeen, the balance of twenty. May he be built by her. And th[i]rty will be delayed, "on his neck."  
  6. Manṣūr b. Isaac, her guardian, [accepted for her this ring of security. She is a minor in his jurisdiction, and he shall pro[vide]  
  7. for her [n]eeds until her marriage to her husband. And they are obligated to observe the stipulations of the congregation according to their laws and regulations. And he accepted, [Samuel]  
  8. [b. Abra]ham, all [this, as it is written and in] its time. And this writ of betrothal is valid. I, Muʿammar b. 
  9. Yaʿqūb Benjamin ... I  
  10. Aaron b. R. ...witness  
  11. … b. Saul ... Yaʿq[ūb b.] Maymūn, witness
  12. ...witness Abraham ha-shofeṭ berrabi

  3. [to the custom of the small synagogue, of the] Babylonians [...]  twenty-five gold pieces, the custom of the congregation 
  4. [...] May he be built by her. And the remainder will be delayed, "on his neck.''

  6. [ ... This writ of betrothal is valid in the l]aws (courts) of Israel.
