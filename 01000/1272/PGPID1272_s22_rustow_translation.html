Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S Ar.53.51

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto, right side

  1. What I have transfered for collection (via ḥawāla) from the shipowners and the advance payments from their owners,
  2. and what I left in the warehouses in the hands of my lord the elder Abū Saʿīd Khallūf b.
  3. Zakariyā al-Ashqar, may God support him — from that:
  4. Qāsim b. Zāʾī: 28 bales of Mīsārī flax, of which one load is of the baḥrī (northern/maritime) type.
  5. That comes to 9 loads plus thirty. I had with him one load of baḥrī pepper.
  6. That makes 10 loads plus thirty. He collected from them a salfa (shipping fee) of 19 dinars plus one-half, one-quarter, a qīrāṭ, and a ḥabba. 
  7. I had with him a box of sukk (a compound medication from South Asia); he collected a dinar from its salfa. What his partner
  8. Aḥmad b. Sundalām collected from my lord the elder in Fustat: 5 dinars. The total of that:
  9. 25 dinars, one-quarter, one-sixth, one-eighth, and a ḥabba.
  10. I transferred via ḥawāla to ʿUmar b. Jawkal to collect 17 bales of flax, of which 6
  11. bales are of the baḥrī type, and I myself collected from him 19 bales of the barrī (land) type and one load
  12. of pepper — 14 loads in all. What is to be collected from him from what arrived (accrued?)
  13. on his side: 25 dinars and one-third.
  14. I transferred via ḥawāla  to Abū ʿAlī b. al-Jazūla to collect 16 bales of flax
  15. at various freight rates, the details of which are registered with him from what I wrote to him.
  16. He must collect from it what has accrued with him from the salfa: 15 dinars less
  17. a qīrāt and a ḥabba.
  18. I transferred via ḥawāla to Ibn al-Qaddār to collect half the salfa for twenty baḥrī loads
  19. at various freight rates: 32 dinars. What he collected from my lord the
  20. elder, may God perpetuate his glory, in Fustat: 4 dinars, one-half and two-thirds. From me personally: a dinar.

Recto, left side

  1. The total of what accrued on his side: 37 dinars, one-half, and one-third. I collected
  2. from him the total of what I had loaded with him of flax and other goods.
  3. I transferred via ḥawāla to ʿAbdūn al-Nashāʾ to collect from him 18 barrī bales,
  4. that is, 6 baḥrī loads. He is to collect from him from what accrued on his side from
  5. the salfa: 11 dinars.
  6. I assigned to Abū Isḥaq, the associate of the elder Abū l-Faraj b. ʿAllān,
  7. to collect from the total of what he loaded in the boat of Ibn Yadar: 3 barrī bales.
  8. I left 51 bales in his hands in the warehouse. What is to be collected
  9. from the shipowners, from the number of bales mentioned above: 82.
  10. The total of all that: 133 bales and a box of sukk.
  11. For my lord the elder Abū Yaʿqūb, may God perpetuate his glory, I left
  12. in the warehouse a bundle of flax weighing sixty raṭls, being what remained to me
  13. from the bale I carried with me overland.
  14. I transferred via ḥawāla to Ibn al-Jazūla, so that he has no claim on me for the bales for porters'
  15. entry fees, nor for warehouse storage fees, nor for debt — but he is entitled to the warehouse rental fee from the time of
  16. [their] entry [until the time when] he collects them. Likewise, I transferred via ḥawāla to Ibn
  17. Jawkal [regarding] the 17 remaining bales with him, so that he has no claim on them for
  18. porters' entry, nor for debt, nor for storage, nor warehouse rental —
  19. except for the fee for the month of Shaʿban of the year 429.

Verso, right side

  1. ... and four hundred (i.e., the year 429?), for 17 discounted/damaged bales. He has collected from me everything that
  2. I owed on that account in transport fees, and likewise for the warehouses. I have paid
  3. him the rental up to the middle of Sha'ban of
  4. the year 429. I left with him 10 dinars 
  5. to spend on this cargo, and I lent him 5 dinars, which he may use
  6. for my lord the elder, may God perpetuate his glory.
