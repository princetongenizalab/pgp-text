English Translation of Legal document: T-S 13J3.3

S. D. Goitein, see scan.

  1. This testimony was given in our presence, we, the witnesses signing at the end of this will. This happened on
  2. Wednesday, the 26th lyyar of the year 1454
  3. of the era of the documents (= April 13, 1143), in Fustat, which is under the authority of our lord Shemuʾel, the great Nagid, may his name endure forever. We entered
  4. the house of the elder Abū l-Mūna, the druggist, the Alexandrian, and found his daughter Sitt al-Ahl, the wife of
  5. Abū Naṣr of Aleppo, the merchant, ill and confined to her bed - after having properly taken cognizance of her.
  6. She was in possession of her faculties and made her declaration in clear language and with
  7. sound mind. This is what she said to us: When my father gave me this large house in the Ḥabs Banān 
  8. a year and a half ago, a document about this gift was made out before Muslim authorities. But before this he imposed on me the
  9. condition that he, my mother, and my brother, the elder Abu l-Surūr, should never be forced to leave the upper floor, as long as they lived in this world. (Above the line: My father should stay in that apartment as long as he lived.)
  10. I wish now that this stipulation should be carried out unchanged, for it was made in the
  11. presence of my husband, the elder Abū Naṣr. When that which is ordained for me will come to pass, I wish to be buried
  12. in this house in which I am now and not be carried out (to the cemetery) except when one of my family dies, I mean my
  13. father, mother, or brother. Then I might be carried out together with the one who will die. The maidservant Fūq does not belong to him (her husband); but my mother
  14. gave me money with which I bought her. She has a daughter, who belongs to my mother, not to me. My boy shall stay
  15. with my mother as he does now. No one shall separate him from them. I receive (from my husband) 2 1/2 dinars
  16. per month for expenses, which makes 30 dinars a year, as well as 12 irdabb wheat. Of this, I relinquish to him (the husband)
  17. 4 irdabb and half a dinar per month, so that he will have to pay to my son
  18. Mūsā 2 dinars every month and 8 irdabb wheat a year. The rent for the
  19. middle floor, where Hiba lives, will be to my mother until my husband returns. I possess a
  20. document assigning the rent to me during his absence. I wish to have a (new) burial attire, since I have none with
  21. which I am satisfied. There should be bought for me a robe of fine Dabiqi linen with a hood, a mantle, and a cloak, and nisāfi material
  22. for the burial couch and braid. All this to cost about 25 dinars. And a coffin costing 9-10
  23. dinars. Wailing should be done by Muslims. I wish to have the daughter of my brother Abū l-Surur for my boy Mūsā.
  24. After having made all these declarations in our presence, she appointed as her executor our master Aharon haKohen, the honored [elder],
  25. son of our master Yeshuʿa, haKohen, the elder, (may he)(rest in) E(den), concerning all this and concerning her will,
  26. and [she] said: "He is my representative] after my death and my executor also with regard to my father and mother." When [we heard what she said]
  27. we wrote it down on the aforementioned date, so that our testimony should serve as a document conveying rights.
  28. The word... is erased and should not be considered. All the rest is confirmed. She accepted the aforementioned Abū Mūsā Hārūn, the Kohen, as trustworthy
  29. as two witnesses admitted in court with regard to all his actions in matters of her will. Of this the (expenditure for) the seven days
  30. of mourning for me to be taken from the expenses due me, since he is the executor for the son of his brother. Everything is correct, valid, and confirmed.
  31. She further declared in our presence that she kept nothing (belonging to anyone else) in her home. She also said: I release my mother, my father, and my brother
  32. Abu l-Surūr from all claims and suits which may be brought against them in my name. No one is entitled to molest them by asking them to give an oath
  33. of any kind. Confirmed. Written between the lines: "She said I" Confirmed.
  34. Also written between the lines: "My father should stay in that apartment as long as he lived." Everything is confirmed.
  35. Yosef ha-Levi, son of Thābit, (may he) r(est in) E(den). Shelomo, son of Natan he-ḥaver, (may he) r(est in) E(den).
