Verso
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
The ʾamīr ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Ḥusayn the qāʾid
(Praise be to God, through him is my success)
has received, out of a debt of nine dīnārs, in the year
five-hundred and seven, the dues of six months (reckoning at)
eighteen and a half waraq dirhams (a month).
He wrote (this) on the aforementioned date.
