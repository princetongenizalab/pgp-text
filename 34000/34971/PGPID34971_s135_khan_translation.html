English Translation of Credit instrument or private receipt: T-S Ar.43.54

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Verso

  1. In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate.
  2. The ʾamīr ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Ḥusayn the qāʾid
  3. (Praise be to God, through him is my success)
  4. has received, out of a debt of nine dīnārs, in the year
  5. five-hundred and seven, the dues of six months (reckoning at)
  6. eighteen and a half waraq dirhams (a month).
  7. He wrote (this) on the aforementioned date.
