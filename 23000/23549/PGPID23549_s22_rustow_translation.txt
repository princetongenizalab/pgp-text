Verso
When you reach the Fayyūm, ask
about a village (qarya) called al-Kharba (al-Khirba?),
which is one of the districts (aʿmāl) of the Fayyūm. When
you get there, ask about the synagogue (or church: kanīsa)
of the village that is located there. It is
outside the village. Among the signs (by which you will recognize)
this kanīsa: next to it there is
a lote tree (sidra) and a date palm. In its wall,
in the middle, there is a stone that is part of the masonry. In this
stone is a hole (filled with) lead. Measure from the stone
toward the outside of the wall two cubits, no
more, and dig. You will find stone
and masonry, and under it, a copper jar filled with
Byzantine dinars (danānīr rūmiyya). Another description:
In al-Kharba, look at the bathhouse
. . . in it. Look behind its back (wall).
