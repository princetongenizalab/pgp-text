Recto, left side
Let that be granted.
The exalted and noble ordinance of al-Ẓāfir, the benedictions of God be upon  him, was given
to instruct al-Hāfiẓ's noble warehouse of books,
may God double its light, to issue the epistle of the worthy
elder, ʿAlī ibn ʿĪsa, for the ʿamīr Faḵr al-Dīn
ʾAbū Manṣūr ʿIzz al-ʿArab ibn Muḥammad, described as (the one of) the  šurbūš bonnets,
one of the entourage of the exalted presence, by means of the issuing of
a noble minute with the form 'let that be granted',
after seven days had passed of Ṣafar, in the year five-hundred and thirty-seven,
as a benevolent act.
Our sufficiency is God. How excellent a keeper is he!
Recto, left side, right margin
It was registered in the capital. 
Praise be to God alone.
