English Translation of State document: T-S Ar.40.41

Geoffrey Khan, Arabic Legal and Administrative Documents in the Cambridge Genizah Collections (Cambridge University Press, 1993).

Recto, left side

  1. Let that be granted.
  2. The exalted and noble ordinance of al-Ẓāfir, the benedictions of God be upon  him, was given
  3. to instruct al-Hāfiẓ's noble warehouse of books,
  4. may God double its light, to issue the epistle of the worthy
  5. elder, ʿAlī ibn ʿĪsa, for the ʿamīr Faḵr al-Dīn
  6. ʾAbū Manṣūr ʿIzz al-ʿArab ibn Muḥammad, described as (the one of) the  šurbūš bonnets,
  7. one of the entourage of the exalted presence, by means of the issuing of
  8. a noble minute with the form 'let that be granted',
  9. after seven days had passed of Ṣafar, in the year five-hundred and thirty-seven,
  10. as a benevolent act.
  11. Our sufficiency is God. How excellent a keeper is he!

Recto, left side, right margin

  1. It was registered in the capital.
  2. Praise be to God alone.
