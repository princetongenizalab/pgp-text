(I) Joseph b. Perahyā - may he rest in Eden! - known as Yijū, Mevassēr's brother, humbly beg
my lord's generosity to allow me to devote myself to you and to be included in the assembly of your clients [...]

What I request of your benevolence and hope from your graciousness, is that you kindly write
in detail to your servant [...] the amount of the goods sent [...]



He wrote that part of it had been lost. I remain anxious and troubled
by every aspect. For my brother had written that he determined that the capital, which remained in Fustat after
most had been lost was 22 Egyptian dinar, from which was purchased pepper for us,
together. Then he said [= wrote] \\to me//: "The total of your half is 64 pounds of pepper and 22
Ducan rubāʿis." But my agent says: "Only 65 rubāʿis were given me." The verse "What the cutter has left,
the locust has devoured" has been fulfilled in me, as they say, "Poverty follows the poor." For Time has shot its arrows at me.
I have become impoverished and fate has struck me down. [...]
