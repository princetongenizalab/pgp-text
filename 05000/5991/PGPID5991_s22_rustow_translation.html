Unspecified (Unpublished Transcriptions) Translation of Letter: T-S 8.255

Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Recto

  1. [I am writing, O my lord and mas]ter, [may G]od p[rolong] your life, [perpetua]te your support, [your] w[ellbeing, your happines]s, and your benefaction, from Būṣīr,
  2. [having arrived fro]m Dandīl with 11 days [r]emaning in Kislev, and in a state of wellbeing, benefaction, [and ... ], praise be to God, Lord of the universe. I inform my lord that his letter has arrived,
  3. along with his kindness of the six dīnā(rs) with which he so graciously favored me, and the installment [paid by] Abū Saʿīd. [May God p]ut them in
  4. the realm of safety along with all Israel. My lord mentioned that he had graced me with collecting the price of the wax through my lord my paternal uncle. He mentioned his arrangement
  5. with him: he continues to be gracious. He mentioned that his heart inclines him to say there is no solution, but when he reads my letter he will do something about what they did, God willing.
  6. My lord mentioned that [ ... ] — and it is surprising that my lord would instruct me [ ... ]. He mentioned in his first letter, which reached
  7. me, that I should not buy him even a single carob-weight's worth on credit (salaf). But by God, let it be known that I bought in the settlement (balad) a full qinṭ(ār) of flax
  8. on commenda (muqāraḍa). He mentioned [ ... ] my dīnārs, all of them Shāmī. By God, I only bought from the vagabonds (ṣaʿālik) less than 100 dīn(ārs)
  9. which I paid them for [ ... ]. \\ Also, by God, [ ... ] // all my purchases from those two I conveyed with a bill of exchange (suftaja), and from him (?). By God, I did not weigh it out to the tax-cashier (jahbadh)
  10. [ ... ] for it; he took it from me. What is more, by God, what possessed me to buy it from the vagabonds (ṣaʿālīk)
  11. [ ... ] some of what the official (kātib) collected in Naẓāriyya dīnārs. By God, the minting (ḍarb) in Fusṭāṭ and the exchange rate (al-ṣarf)
  12. between the dir(ham and) the dīn(ār) [ ... ] 30 dir(hams) per dīn(ār). I have informed my lord of this, and I ask him to endeavor to collect the price of the silk on our behalf.
  13. By God, he knows the situation [ ... ]; its price is the highest possible. I want my lord to [ ... ] and collect its price along with \\ what is held by // the son of my lord
  14. Nissī[m], may God protect him. I am writing to my paternal uncle and pressing him urgently [ ... ]. My lord, perhaps God will facilitate
  15. the collection of all the dīnārs [ ... ]. As for [ ... ] the small one(s) (fem.), I said perhaps they are
  16. slightly unripe, or there is some Naẓāriyya defect in it. I found nothing in it [ ... ], and I am sending it as it is
