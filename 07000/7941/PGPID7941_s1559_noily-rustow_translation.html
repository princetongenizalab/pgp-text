Verso

  1. his honor, concerning what my lord my brother sent for the carrying of Rabbēnū Nissīm, may the memory of the righteous be blessed,
  2. how it was transferred on his behalf/diverted away from him, may God perpetuate [his glory]. What I say about this is that
  3. my lord is my brother; and as for me, there did not appear to us on the part of the elder Abū Zikrī Ibn
  4. Qayyomā, may God perpetuate his support, toward my lord the elder, may God preserve
  5. his days, anything but friendship, praise, and affection; and neither of us thought
  6. that there was between them (dual) a rift that, nor that either of them (dual) was separating from the other
  7. in anything concerning us so that I; rather, their (dual) opinion was shared regarding the carrying of
  8. Rabbēnū Nissīm, of holy and blessed memory, and other things. Likewise, I intended, in charging I thought, when I assigned/commissioned the aforementioned (person)
  9. \\may God perpetuate his honor, to buy for me//
  10. the purchase of whatever responsa (teshuvot) and the like might come to hand, that this \\too// would be too
  11. with the participation of my lord’s judgment and his partnership. So let him not attribute (?) attribute to us, may
  12. God perpetuate his honor, neglect of his due. For the elder Abū Zikrī, even if he is our friend
  13. and our brother and dear to us—my lord the elder, may God perpetuate his support, is the head
  14. of our house and family, and occupies for us the place of a father . . . . . . . . to us . . . . .
