English Translation of Legal document: T-S 16.71

Mordechai Akiva Friedman, Jewish Marriage in Palestine: A Cairo Geniza Study (Tel Aviv University, Chaim Rosenberg School of Jewish Studies, 1980), vol. 2 (The Ketubba Texts).

  1. 1. [...] And the elder[s ...] and prosper.  
  2. 2. (a) [... He who finds a wife fin]ds good / [and obtains favor] of the Lord.  (b) I will greatly rejoice in the Lord; My soul shall rejoice / in my God;  For He has clothed me with garments of salvation, etc.  (c) And all the people that were in the gates / and the elders, said: "We are witnesses,” etc.  
  4. [...] And He who uplifts them will adorn them with joyful occasions. He will make them glad and rejoice. / He will delight them in their marriage (or: in their ingathering). 
  5. [COLUMN A, lines 5-11.] Joy is fixed / and blessing is impressed / and pleasure is eternally bound / and exultation delighted / [and] good in abundance / [to] the esteemed [groo]m / [and to] the modest [bride]. [COLUMN B, lines 5-13.] In the name of my Creator and my Maker / my strength, my refuge and my fortress / my trust from my youth / may He remember me for good / and in His salvation adorn me / and make great my adornment / and bandage my fracture / and make for me a diadem of His strength / and crown me with His crown. [COLUMN C, lines 5-15.] In the rejoicing of groom and bride / He will bedeck me and strengthen me / in might and happiness / He will delight me and give me / He will make my glory grow / and my light shine / And may He bless the respected groom, the kohen / and the modest bride / who are married / on the Sabbath of “When you enter” and the section “Arise, shine.”
