Marina Rustow, unpublished.

Verso, right side

  1. Details of what is charged against the dyed silk:
  2. Original cost: raw silk, 66 raṭls — price: 300 dinars
  3. Expenses and sundries charged to it, as follows:
  4. First: shrinkage on the 66 raṭls — 20 dinars
  5. Kermes (qirmiz) for dyeing — 85 dinars
  6. All further expenses for it — 25 dinars
  7. Wages of the [three] dyers — 20 dinars
  8. Dyeing in black and green — 24 dinars
  9. That subtotal: 174 dinars
  10. The two totals combined: 474 dinars
  11. Net yield of dyed silk: 63 raṭls

Verso, left side

  1. Weight of the silk I left with my master Abū
  2. Isḥāq ibn al-Iskandarānī — may God preserve him:
  3. 17 raṭls [notation unclear]

Recto, left side

  1. I bought safflower (ʿuṣfur): 5 qinṭārs at the price of
  2. 29 dinars per qinṭār — the total price: 145 dinars
  3. Expenses from it: wrapping cloths, a sack, rope, and thread,
  4. two bindings — 8 dāniqs and 2 dirhams — and a gratuity,
  5. porters and sundries: 12 dirhams. That [ ] total:
  6. 154 dinars, 6 dirhams

  8. leaving owed to me 1¼ mithqāls, which he should pay
  9. to him. ʿAṭīq [ ] — I paid it to him.

  11. [ ] and a box of coral — from which ʿAmrān gets
  12. 4 Byzantine coins, and the [ ] is his, and the rest is mine. I paid
