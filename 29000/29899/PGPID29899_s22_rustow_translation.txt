Verso, right side
Details of what is charged against the dyed silk:
Original cost: raw silk, 66 raṭls — price: 300 dinars
Expenses and sundries charged to it, as follows:
First: shrinkage on the 66 raṭls — 20 dinars
Kermes (qirmiz) for dyeing — 85 dinars
All further expenses for it — 25 dinars
Wages of the [three] dyers — 20 dinars
Dyeing in black and green — 24 dinars
That subtotal: 174 dinars
The two totals combined: 474 dinars
Net yield of dyed silk: 63 raṭls

Recto, left side
I bought safflower (ʿuṣfur): 5 qinṭārs at the price of
29 dinars per qinṭār — the total price: 145 dinars
Expenses from it: wrapping cloths, a sack, rope, and thread,
two bindings — 8 dāniqs and 2 dirhams — and a gratuity,
porters and sundries: 12 dirhams. That [ ] total:
154 dinars, 6 dirhams
Recto, right side
What I have aboard a ship: [8?] [ ] — 3 bales
of copper, and a bale of al-qārib [ ] — 2 loads. The freight:
2½ mithqāls. Also a bale of silk,
containing [ ] silk and a bag of dyed goods,
and 2 bundles for ʿAṭīq, and a large cloth/carpet —
reckoned at half a load: freight 1¼ mithqāls.
He received from that 10 dāniqs, which equals 2 mithqāls —
leaving owed to me 1¼ mithqāls, which he should pay
to him. ʿAṭīq [ ] — I paid it to him.
I also have, in the hands of ʿAmrān the Sicilian, a bale containing
[ ] and a box of coral — from which ʿAmrān gets
4 Byzantine coins, and the [ ] is his, and the rest is mine. I paid
for the expenses of the bale — porterage and gratuity — 4 dirhams.
