Recto
Let it be registered on behalf of the office of supervision,               Let it be registered in the office of accounts,
if God wills.                     if God wills.
They have registered the amount of two and a half. 
Praise be to God, with the praise of those who give thanks.
In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate. 
A valid receipt has been granted to the warehouse-keeper in the province of al-Fayyūm on behalf of ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ibn Wahb the estate-manager
for the year four-hundred and three, under the supervision of the auspicious, rightly guided judge, Ṯiqat al-Mulk Makīn al-Dawla wa-ʾAmīnuhā,
the protégé of the commander of the faithful, ʾAbū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī ibn Bahār, with ʾAbū al-Sarī Theodor ibn Yuḥannis acting as a accountant.
Half of this: One and a quarter. Its amount: Two and a half.
Written by Mīḵāʾīl ibn ʿAbd al-Masīḥ, the cashier. Valid. The tenth of Ṣafar
in the year four-hundred and four.
