English Translation of Letter: T-S 8J17.12

Mark R. Cohen, The Voice of the Poor in the Middle Ages (Princeton University Press, 2005).

  1. In the name of the Compassionate
  2. “Behold, God is my salvation,” et[c.]
  3. The most illustrious master the c(antor), the e[lder] (may) God b(e your succor),
  4. is hereby informed that we [. . .]
  5. since [the tim]e of the poll-tax payments [. . .]
  6. in you [. . .] with him [. . .]
  7. that the bearer of this has with him. [Sta]nd
  8. by [him and a]ssist him and release them
  9. to him [imm]ediately, and do not make it neccesary [for us]
  10. to reprove you. Appear with [him]
  11. at once, with the money in hand.
  12. Beware of being disobedient.
  13. May God the ex(alted) be [your succor]
  14. and may He watch over you. And peace.
