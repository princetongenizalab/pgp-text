English Translation of Letter: T-S Ar.39.23

Yusuf Umrethwala and Alan Elbaum, unpublished translations (n.p., 2024).

Recto

  1. As for the firewood, it has reached [Minyat] al-Qays, 30 loads at the price of
  2. 3 ¼ dirhams per load, and all of the remaining firewood
  3. was sent to Minbāl, at the price of 2 dirhams per load. Let it be transported to the Nile.
  4. And peace.
  5. And 10 loads were sent, and the masters of the ships are protesting because of all the mud,
  6. and when the water level rises, the ships will float well. Send the rest, which is in [Minyat] al-Qays. . .
