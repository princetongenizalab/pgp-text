English Translation of Letter: T-S Ar.39.435

Jane Hathaway, Ottoman-Era Documents from the Cairo Genizah (Open Book Publishers, 2026).

Recto, main text

  1. [    ] To my most honoured, esteemed, noble, dear father //al-Ḥājj Muḥammad//,

  2. [    ] ...after offering greetings and sincere prayers, now then:

  3. My dear father, this time, 275 qinṭārs of firewood were sent with the navigator ʿAbdallāh Anṣārī Saʿīdī.

  4. When they arrive, take possession of them.

  5. My dear father, in this manner

  6. take possession of them and record them in your register,

  7. and the Mevlevī dervishes

  8. on the occasion of the navigator’s sojourn there [lit., ‘on that side’]

  9. ...

Recto, bottom, middle of the page

  1. ʿAbdallāh Anṣārī Saʿīdī

  2. [price] per [qinṭār]         firewood

  3. 27 [pāras?]                      qinṭārs

  4.                                           27[5] [    ]

  5. [total:                        7425]

  6. [shipp]ing [cost]s

Recto, right margin, upside-down

  1. And my dear father [    ]

  2. says, ‘We will send the things, God, the exalted, willing.

  3. We haven’t forgotten them.’

  4. Greeting[s] to Sayyid Muḥammad and Sayyid ʿUmar [who are] over there.
