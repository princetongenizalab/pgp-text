English Translation of Letter: T-S AS 178.88

Jane Hathaway, Ottoman-Era Documents from the Cairo Genizah (Open Book Publishers, 2026).

Recto, main text

  1. My petition to the noble dust under the feet of his excellency is as follows:

  2. There are places that produce income for al-Khaḍra muqāṭaʿa. Various kāshifs’ men and other people come from everywhere

  3. to this region and take the sheep and other objects that be- long to the state,

  4. and in addition to taking them, they even say of the peasants’ sheep and possessions, ‘They are ours’, and in this way

  5. they make off with the māl-i mīrī. When we ask them why

    they are doing this, some pull out a mace, some a sword,

  6. and cause harm to the māl-i mīrī. From my lord is requested a definitive noble decree, confirming that no one from among these [people] may obstruct the māl-i mīrī,

  7. that they may not pull a sword or mace on us, and that they must not prevent us from taking the property that belongs to us,

  8. [so] that the māl-i mīrī is collected and not harmed,

  9. and that no one who has māl-i mīrī in his possession, whoever he may be, shall contravene the [sacred] law.

  10. The eternal command is my lord’s.

Recto, signature

Recto, top left
